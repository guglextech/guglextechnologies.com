USSD is a session, not a website. It will time out if you pause too long. Have the meter number, smartcard, or phone number ready before you dial.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A network signal.
2. The destination identifier written down.
3. Your wallet PIN.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Choose Telco, Education, Utilities, TV Bills, or Support.
3. Enter details. Confirm names and numbers.
4. Approve the separate PIN prompt.

## After you pay

When the USSD screen vanishes, the PIN prompt may still be alive. Check the other message. Check wallet history before retrying.

## If something fails

- **Session timeout:** Start again. Look for a debit first.
- **No signal:** Move, wait, or try the web when you have data.

Any GSM phone. Any of the live networks. One shortcode.
