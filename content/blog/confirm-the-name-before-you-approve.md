After PIN, the sale is real. Before PIN, the name on the screen is a gift. Use it. If you do not recognise the name, you are not “almost done” — you are about to pay the wrong destination.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Someone who knows the registered name.
2. Willingness to abort.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Enter the identifier.
2. Read the name aloud.
3. Abort if it is wrong. There is no prize for finishing a bad session.

## After you pay

A cancelled USSD session is free. A completed wrong sale is a support adventure.

## If something fails

- **Close enough spelling:** If it is a different household, stop even if the surname is similar.
- **No name shown:** Then confirm the number itself digit by digit.

Names are a safety rail. Lean on them.
