Old results still need a live voucher. The year dropdown on WAEC is what points at 2023 or 2024, not the date you bought on Ewale.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The index from that year.
2. A WASSCE checker from **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**).

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Buy WASSCE / Nov-Dec today on **\*714\*22#**.
2. On [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/) set the **exam year you sat**, not today’s year by habit.
3. Submit serial and PIN.

## After you pay

Wrong year is a common “result not found”. Fix the year before buying another PIN.

## If something fails

- **Used a 2019 index with 2026 selected:** Change the year. Keep the voucher.

New voucher, old year, same official portal.
