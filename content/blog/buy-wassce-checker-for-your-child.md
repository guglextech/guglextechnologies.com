A WASSCE checker is not school fees and not admission. It is paid access to the grade list. Buy it when the candidate is ready to type the index.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. WASSCE, not BECE.
2. Child’s index number.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Buy on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**).
2. Send a photo of the SMS if the child is on campus.
3. Child checks on [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/).
4. Parent keeps a copy of the screenshot.

## After you pay

Universities will want official processes later. This printout is for you and the candidate today.

## If something fails

- **WhatsApp “I can check for you”:** Do not give PIN and index to a stranger. Check together.

You pay. They type the index. WAEC shows the subjects.
