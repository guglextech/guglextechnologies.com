Failed PIN for insufficient funds means nothing was bought. That is good. What is not good is topping up too little, failing twice, then succeeding on a third try while a relative also pays.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The amount on the **current** confirmation screen.
2. A small buffer for fees if the screen includes them.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Read the total.
2. Fund the wallet to that total.
3. Run **\*714\*22#** once.

## After you pay

A declined insufficient-funds attempt is not a pending token. You may retry after funding.

## If something fails

- **Ignoring the fee line:** If the screen shows a fee, that is part of the debit.
- **Partial wallet plus agent cash:** Reconcile so only one path completes the bill.

Fund to the summary total. Then one PIN.
