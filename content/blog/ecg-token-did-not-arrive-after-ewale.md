Tokens are generated after settlement. The USSD screen often closes before the SMS. That gap is where people panic and pay twice — two tokens, one house that only needed one.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. MoMo history showing the debit.
2. Time.
3. The paying number’s SMS inbox.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Wait. Night and weekend traffic still settles, just not always in ten seconds.
2. Search SMS for numbers that look like a token.
3. If it is truly missing, use Ewale Support on **\*714\*22#** or **walletewale@gmail.com** with the reference.

## After you pay

When the delayed SMS arrives, enter that token. Ignore the instinct to buy “backup units.”

## If something fails

- **Full inbox:** Delete old bank messages and look again.
- **Second purchase already done:** You now have two tokens. Use both on the same meter if they belong to it.

Recover the first token. Only buy again if the first debit reversed.
