Both channels sell the same classes of product. Neither is a different company. If you start on web and finish on USSD, you can still double-pay — the channels do not lock each other out.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. An honest look at your signal and battery.
2. The same identifiers you would type on USSD.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Pick **one** channel for this payment.
2. Complete it. Wait for SMS.
3. Only then consider the other channel for a different bill.

## After you pay

Web is nicer for long smartcard numbers. USSD is nicer in a power cut with a keypad phone. The receipt is still SMS.

## If something fails

- **Started on both:** Check history. You may already have two successes.
- **Browser failed mid-pay:** Check MoMo. Do not immediately dial USSD for the same bill until you know the web debit failed.

One channel, one debit, one SMS. Then stop.
