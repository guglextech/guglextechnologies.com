Vendors are convenient until they are sold out. Your shortcode is not sold out.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. MoMo.
2. Index number.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**, buy BECE.
2. Open the official BECE checker.
3. Enter serial, PIN, index.

## After you pay

You can still print at a vendor. You do not have to **buy** at a vendor.

## If something fails

- **Vendor offered to “do it all” for extra:** Pay extra only if you want their labour, not a second voucher you already own.

Vendor optional. Official check required.
