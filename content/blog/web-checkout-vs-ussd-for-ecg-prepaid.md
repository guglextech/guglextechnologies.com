This blog is a third surface that does not take money. Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog. Ewale is a checkout in front of ECG’s vending system. It is not Electricity Company of Ghana.

## What the two Ewale surfaces share

Prepaid still wants a meter number. The name still must match. The PIN still belongs on the MoMo prompt (or a card flow if the web page offers one). The SMS token still must be typed on the meter. Pending still means wait. Wrong product still means you paid a bill or a gift instead of lighting this room. Empty prepaid still is not dumsor.

If you learned those rules on USSD, you do not relearn physics on the website. You just see them on a bigger screen.

## When USSD is the better everyday tool

Feature phone. Smartphone with no data. Compound with a 2G corner. PowerApp OTP already failing so you do not want another browser OTP. You can recite **\*714\*22#** without looking at a bookmark. Session risk is the cost: drops, fat-finger menus, screens that vanish. Finish, then read the wallet if you are unsure.

USSD is also how you teach a parent who will never install an app. Official USSD **\*226#** is the sister lesson if they only trust ECG’s header.

## When the website is the better everyday tool

You want to see the name clearly. You want to copy a meter number from Notes without retyping eleven digits on a numeric USSD field. You have data. You may have a card. You are paying from another town and want to screenshot the confirmation for the family chat. Use the real [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com), not an ad clone.

Web is worse when data is the thing that died with the lights, or when the only adult left in the house has a Nokia.

## Do not mix Ewale surfaces with official surfaces in one pending hour

Website pending, then **\*714\*22#** “because USSD is more reliable,” then ECG PowerApp “because it is ECG,” is three checkouts. Pick one surface, then one backup only after the ledger is quiet. Official ECG PowerApp is not a web twin of Ewale. Official **\*226#** is not a USSD twin of Ewale. They are ECG’s shops. Ewale is the everyday shop this site prefers.

Faults, meter hardware, reconnection: **0302 611 611**, regardless of which page you like.

## Screenshots, bookmarks, and shared phones

Bookmark [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) yourself. Do not trust a link from a group named “ECG tokens Accra.” On a shared phone, log out of any card wallet after you pay. Do not pay both surfaces to see which name is “more official.” Names come from the same class of lookup. Your job is to match the house, not to A/B test checkouts.

Web checkout is not PowerApp. If you wanted ECG’s official graphical door, that is ECG PowerApp, with OTP and ECG’s own receipts. Mixing “I paid on the website so I am in the app” is how people hunt tokens in the wrong inbox.
