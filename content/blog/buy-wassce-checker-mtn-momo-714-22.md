Most WASSCE searches on results week happen on MTN phones. You do not need the Ewale app. The dialler is enough.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. An **MTN** number with MoMo balance for the amount shown.
2. WASSCE selected — SHS, not JHS.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. On the MTN phone, dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Results Vouchers → **WASSCE / Nov-Dec**.
3. Self or other, then quantity.
4. Approve the MTN MoMo prompt.

## After you pay

Open SMS before you check WhatsApp. The voucher is easy to miss in a busy inbox.

## If something fails

- **Prompt on a different SIM:** Dual-SIM phones: the wallet that was billed is the one that must show the MoMo SMS.

MTN line, one PIN, one WASSCE voucher. Then ghana.waecdirect.org.
