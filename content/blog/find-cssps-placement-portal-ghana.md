Every September, families mix “results” and “placement”. CSSPS tells you the school. The BECE checker tells you the grades. Different websites, different payments.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. BECE index number and date of birth as registered.
2. The official site [cssps.gov.gh](https://www.cssps.gov.gh/).

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Open [cssps.gov.gh](https://www.cssps.gov.gh/) — not a Facebook image of a portal.
2. Enter index and year the way CSSPS labels (often index + year).
3. Do **not** type an Ewale serial here.
4. If you still need grades, buy a **BECE** checker on **\*714\*22#** and use the WAEC site.

## Is placement free?

In recent years GES has allowed free online placement checks. If a page sells a “CSSPS PIN”, confirm it is really required this cycle. A BECE result checker will not log you into CSSPS.

## If something fails

- **Index not found:** Fix digits and date of birth before paying anyone.

cssps.gov.gh for school. WAEC for grades. Ewale for the grade voucher.
