Airtime is instant. DSTV often is not. The decoder must be on, plugged in, and looking at the sky (or cable) it always uses. A second Ewale payment will not reboot a decoder that is unplugged.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The SMS receipt.
2. The decoder powered on.
3. A clock.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Confirm the debit in MoMo.
2. Leave the box on for 15–30 minutes.
3. Reboot the decoder once if the menu allows.
4. If still dead, Multichoice and Ewale support with the receipt.

## After you pay

A successful Ewale sale that Multichoice has not applied yet is a support job. A second sale is a second month.

## If something fails

- **Dish problem:** Payment cannot fix a shifted dish. Check hardware.
- **Wrong smartcard:** You restored a different decoder. That box may now be fine.

Receipt, power, wait, support. Duplicate pay is last, not first.
