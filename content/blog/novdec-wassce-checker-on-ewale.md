Nov/Dec is still WASSCE, with a private sitting. Ewale does not sell a third mystery SKU. You buy WASSCE / Nov-Dec, then tell WAEC which sitting.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Your **private** index number.
2. The year you sat Nov/Dec.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#** → WASSCE / Nov-Dec.
2. Pay once. Save SMS.
3. On [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/) choose WASSCE Private / Nov-Dec, not School.

## After you pay

A school-candidate index will not open a private sitting. The voucher is not the mistake — the form is.

## If something fails

- **Bought BECE because it was cheaper in a shop:** Wrong exam. Buy WASSCE / Nov-Dec on Ewale.

Same voucher family as school WASSCE. Different index and exam type on WAEC.
