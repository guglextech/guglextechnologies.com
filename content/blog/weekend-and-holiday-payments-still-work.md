Some billers post slower on holidays. The shortcode still accepts the payment. Your job is still not to triple-tap because a Sunday SMS is late.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The same identifiers as a weekday.
2. Slightly more wait time.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Pay as usual on **\*714\*22#**.
2. Wait longer than a weekday instinct.
3. Use support if fulfilment never comes — with the reference.

## After you pay

ECG tokens, airtime, and many TV renewals still complete on holidays. Water posting may look lazy. Receipts still matter.

## If something fails

- **Assuming USSD is closed:** Try. If it opens, it is open.
- **Holiday panic double pay:** The rule does not relax because it is a public holiday.

Open 24/7 does not mean instant on every biller. It means you can start the payment.
