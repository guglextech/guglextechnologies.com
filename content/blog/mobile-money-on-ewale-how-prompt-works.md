Ewale never needs your PIN typed into a blog comment or a random WhatsApp number. The only PIN box that matters is the official wallet prompt on your SIM.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The wallet SIM in coverage.
2. Enough float for amount plus any displayed fee.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Confirm the Ewale summary.
2. Read the prompt: merchant, amount, number.
3. Approve if it matches. Decline if it does not.
4. Keep the Ewale SMS that follows a success.

## After you pay

A declined prompt is clean. A successful prompt is a sale in progress. Those are the only two states that matter.

## If something fails

- **Prompt on unused SIM:** Wallets are tied to numbers. Swap the paying SIM or use that number’s phone.
- **Someone asking for PIN “to help”:** Hang up. Complete the flow yourself or use official support.

Read the prompt. Approve once. Never share the PIN.
