Three clocks exist: your USSD session, the wallet debit, and the biller (ECG, GWCL, Multichoice, telco). They do not always finish together. Pending is the gap.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The time of the attempt.
2. Wallet history.
3. Willingness to wait minutes, not seconds, on results week or Sunday evenings.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Note whether money left the wallet.
2. If yes, wait for fulfilment (token, airtime, voucher, TV activation).
3. If the wallet reversed, the sale did not complete — then you may retry.

## After you pay

Retrying while pending is the most expensive habit on this blog. It creates twins: two tokens, two DSTV receipts, two checkers.

## If something fails

- **USSD said error after PIN success:** Believe the wallet. Then believe the SMS.
- **Pending all night:** Contact support with the reference rather than looping USSD.

Money out: wait. Money back: retry. Money out twice: you bought twice.
