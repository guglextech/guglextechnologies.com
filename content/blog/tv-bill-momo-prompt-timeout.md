TV amounts are larger than a small airtime sale, so a duplicate hurts more. Timeouts feel like nothing happened. History knows better.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. MoMo / Telecel Cash / AT history.
2. The IUC or smartcard you used.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Do not redial immediately.
2. Open wallet history for a debit to Ewale.
3. If none, retry **TV Bills** once.
4. If a debit exists, wait for SMS and decoder restore.

## After you pay

A timeout after a successful debit is a fulfilment wait. A timeout with no debit is a safe retry.

## If something fails

- **Prompt on the other phone:** The wallet SIM might be in a drawer. Find it before retrying.
- **Approved late, then retried:** Check for two successes.

History first. Second DSTV payment only when the first did not debit.
