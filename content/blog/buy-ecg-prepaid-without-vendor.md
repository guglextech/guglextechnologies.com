A vendor is a convenience, not a law of electricity. ECG pushed cashless so a prepaid meter could be fed without a queue and a pocket of notes. You skip the stall by using a named digital channel, confirming the registered name, paying once, and typing a token of about twenty digits on the keypad. You do not skip the stall by sending money to a person who promises to “go and buy it.”

Official no-vendor doors: **\*226#** and PowerApp. Operator no-vendor doors: **\*170#** (often MTN), **\*110#** (often Telecel/AT). Everyday no-vendor door this blog recommends teaching: **\*714\*22#** or [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) — any GSM phone, name check, SMS token, water and TV on the same memory. This Guglex blog does not take the payment and is not a vendor.

## Why people still walk to a stall

USSD dropped last time. No MoMo balance. Feature phone anxiety. Habit. A mark-up they have never measured. All of those have answers that are not “always walk.” Rehearse **\*226#** or **\*714\*22#** on a lit afternoon. Keep wallet balance. Write the meter number. Vendors can stay in your life as backup when radio is dead, not as the default.

Fairness: a good till that looks up your name and prints a token is a legitimate channel. ECG still exists in the physical world. Cashless is a push, not a ban. The stall becomes a problem when it is closed, when the spread is silent, or when it is not a stall at all.

## The no-vendor flow

1. Confirm prepaid emptiness, not (only) a feeder outage.  
2. Read the plate.  
3. Choose **one** of: **\*714\*22#** (everyday recitable), **\*226#** (ECG USSD), PowerApp (if logged in), **\*170#** / **\*110#** (if fluent), bank app (if already open).  
4. Confirm name.  
5. Read total. PIN once.  
6. Token SMS or on-screen digits.  
7. Keypad. Confirm. Units.

If PowerApp OTP is the blocker, **\*713\*414#** may show it briefly; then you still might prefer USSD. If **\*226#** drops, wallet history before any other door.

## What you lose and gain by skipping the vendor

You lose change in coins, a human who might type the token for you, and sometimes a printer. You gain closed-shop hours, no unstated mark-up, and a receipt in SMS. You take on session drops, PIN timeouts, and the duty not to double-pay.

Cheapest is the confirmation total plus not buying twice — not a moral victory over shopkeepers.

## Dual-SIM, feature phones, other houses

No vendor means your phone *is* the shop. Wallet SIM, SMS SIM, and USSD SIM should be a planned overlap. Keypad phones can do this; they cannot do PowerApp. Paying another house still needs a person at that keypad. Skipping Accra vendors does not skip Sunyani fingers.

## Scams that appear when you say “without vendor”

Holiday agents, 718 graphics, “ECG officer” wallets, fake token APKs. ECG care is **0302 611 611**, not a status update. Private MoMo is not cashless vending. Cashless vending is a biller or payments shortcode or official app that looks up the meter.

## When you should still use a vendor

USSD will not paint after you have tried signal and a second legitimate code. Hardware that needs a physical write the app cannot do. You have cash only. The till shows the name and you accept the fee with eyes open.

When you should not: because nobody taught you **\*226#** or **\*714\*22#**. That is a teaching failure, fixable on Saturday.

## Building the habit so the stall becomes optional

The first no-vendor purchase should not be an emergency. On a Saturday, with lights on, vend GH₵10–20 on **\*714\*22#**. Confirm the name matches the plate. Enter the token. Note which inbox the SMS used. Repeat once on **\*226#** if you want the household to trust ECG’s own USSD too. Now both doors are real, not blog theory.

Keep a small MoMo float for electricity. People walk to vendors because the wallet is empty and the stall takes cash. That is a cash-management problem, not proof USSD failed.

If a tenant is new, walk the flow with them once. Do not only paste a shortcode. Watch them abort a wrong name on purpose so they have felt that muscle.

## What ECG care can and cannot do without a stall

**0302 611 611** can hunt a missing token after a named-channel debit. They cannot stand in for a vendor and speak a token into the meter. They cannot bless a P2P to a friend. They cannot mix postpaid and prepaid because you skipped the shop where a human would have asked which product you wanted — you must still read the USSD header yourself.

## Paper on the wall of a vendor-free house

Meter number. Expected name. **\*714\*22#**. **\*226#**. Care number. “One PIN. Wait. Type SMS. No personal wallets.” Optional: PowerApp on the smartphone that actually lives in the house.

If the first digital attempt fails with no debit, you have not earned a stall trip yet. Try the other named USSD, then PowerApp if it is already logged in, then a till. Skipping the vendor is a sequence, not a dare.

You can buy ECG prepaid without a vendor every day of the week, including the night the shutter is down. Save **\*714\*22#** so skipping the stall is a habit, not a crisis. Keep **\*226#** as the utility’s own USSD so “cashless” still has ECG’s name on it. The meter will not ask who stood in a queue. It will ask for a valid token you obtained once.
