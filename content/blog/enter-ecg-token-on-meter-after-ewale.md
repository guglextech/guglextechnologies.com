The SMS is useless until the meter accepts it. Most rejections are skipped digits, an old token, or standing at a different meter than the one you paid.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The **latest** token SMS.
2. Physical access to the correct keypad.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Turn on or wake the meter display.
2. Enter the digits in order. Do not insert spaces.
3. Press the enter / blue key the meter expects.
4. Wait for units to add. If it errors, stop and compare the display to the SMS.

## After you pay

A rejected token is often still unused. Do not assume you must buy another. Recheck digits first.

## If something fails

- **Old SMS:** Use the token from this payment, not last month’s screenshot.
- **Wrong meter in a compound:** The token is bound to the meter number you paid.

Type what the SMS says, on the meter you confirmed. That is the whole skill.
