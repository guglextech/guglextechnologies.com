Children in Accra pay meters in the hometown every week. The failure mode is using last year’s number. Ewale will show a name. Call the house if that name is a surprise.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The current **meter or account number**, sent as digits, not a voice note you misheard.
2. A quick call to confirm the registered name.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Enter their identifier on **\*714\*22#**.
2. Read the name back to them.
3. Pay. Forward the SMS so they can type a prepaid token or keep a water receipt.

## After you pay

For prepaid, they still must enter the token. Paying is not the same as keying the meter. Send the digits.

## If something fails

- **Landlord changed meters:** Old numbers still work — for the old meter. Get a photo of the new sticker.
- **Two siblings pay the same night:** You just loaded twice. Talk first.

Name match, one debit, forward the SMS. That is remote utility payment.
