If the product is digits, the SMS is the vault. Even bill payments that are not tokens still need a reference when GWCL or ECG is slow to display paid.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Inbox space.
2. A notebook or screenshot folder labelled Ewale.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. After every **\*714\*22#** success, open SMS.
2. Star, screenshot, or copy.
3. Delete it only after the lights are on, the result is checked, or the bouquet is back — and you have a backup.

## After you pay

Support tickets without SMS take longer. You are not helping anyone by “keeping the phone clean” the same night.

## If something fails

- **WhatsApp-only culture:** Forward the SMS to yourself on WhatsApp if you must, but keep the original too.
- **Kids clearing messages:** Move receipts off the shared phone.

Paid digits live in SMS. Guard the thread.
