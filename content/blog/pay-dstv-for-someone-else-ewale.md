Parents renew DSTV for students. Children renew it for parents. The wallet is yours; the subscription is theirs. Communication is part of the payment.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A photo of **their** smartcard.
2. A message that you are paying now.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Announce the payment.
2. Enter their smartcard on **\*714\*22#** DSTV.
3. Forward the SMS receipt.

## After you pay

If they already stood in a queue, you may have stacked two months. That is not a failure — it is a surprise. Talk first.

## If something fails

- **Paid compact onto a disconnected card:** Confirm the decoder still uses that card.
- **Two siblings paid:** Check timestamps. You likely both succeeded.

One family spokesperson, one debit, one forwarded SMS.
