Meter numbers are easy to mistype. Ewale showing a registered name is not decoration. If that name is not your house, the token will still be valid — for them.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Eyes on the **sticker on the meter**, not a number from an old WhatsApp chat.
2. Someone who knows the registered name.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Read the meter number aloud.
2. Enter it on **\*714\*22#**.
3. Read the name that returns. If it is wrong, abort.
4. Only then enter an amount.

## After you pay

A cancelled session before PIN is not a purchase. A confirmed PIN on the wrong meter is a completed gift to that meter.

## If something fails

- **Landlord meter vs your meter:** Pay the meter that serves your lights. Confirm with the people in the house.
- **Shared compound:** Each meter has its own number. Do not recycle a neighbour’s successful number.

Name check first, money second. That order saves tokens.
