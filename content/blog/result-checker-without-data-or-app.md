Purchasing the checker and viewing the result are two steps. Ewale’s step works offline. WAEC’s website usually needs data or a computer lab. Do not confuse those steps.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A GSM phone for **\*714\*22#**.
2. A later plan to use the voucher — school ICT lab, a shop with a PC, or a smartphone with data.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Buy on USSD. Save serial and PIN on paper.
2. When you have a browser, open WAEC’s official checker.
3. Enter index, serial, and PIN.

## After you pay

A paper copy of the voucher survives a stolen or dead phone. The SMS alone is fragile on a shared handset.

## If something fails

- **No data to check results:** The voucher is still valid. Find a browser. Do not buy a second Ewale voucher for that reason alone.
- **App-only rumours:** Ewale USSD is enough to purchase. Apps are optional.

Buy offline. Check when you have a screen. That is the whole design.
