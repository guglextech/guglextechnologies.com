Buying is the easy half. Using the checker is data entry. Most failures are swapped fields, not bad payments.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. SMS in front of you.
2. Index number in front of you.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Open [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/).
2. Index in the index box only.
3. Serial in serial. PIN in PIN (often 12 digits for WASSCE).
4. Year matching the sitting. Submit once.

## After you pay

If it succeeds, you are finished with Ewale for this candidate.

## If something fails

- **Letter O vs zero:** Copy from SMS, do not retype from memory.

Use the voucher on WAEC. Do not type it into random “result apps”.
