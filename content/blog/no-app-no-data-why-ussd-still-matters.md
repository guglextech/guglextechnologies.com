App stores assume battery, data, and storage. A token at 9 p.m. in a village assumes none of that. USSD is the accessible path in the Ewale mission: any mobile phone, no smartphone required.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A SIM that can hit the USSD gateway.
2. Identifiers on paper.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. When the app will not load, stop fighting the app.
2. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
3. Complete the same product you would have tapped online.

## After you pay

You can still use the website tomorrow for history. Tonight’s lights, DSTV, or airtime do not have to wait for a download.

## If something fails

- **Waiting for Wi-Fi to pay ECG:** USSD does not need Wi-Fi. Dial.
- **OS update blocking the app:** The dialler still works.

Accessibility is the point. The shortcode is not a backup; it is a first-class checkout.
