Both vouchers are “result checkers.” Both arrive by SMS. WAEC will still reject a BECE PIN on a WASSCE check. The mix-up happens on the Ewale menu, not at WAEC.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The exam the candidate actually sat this sitting.
2. A slow read of the USSD line that says WASSCE or BECE.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Open Education.
3. Select the exam. If you are unsure, stop and ask the candidate.
4. Only then enter the amount flow and PIN.

## After you pay

A wrong-exam voucher is still a sold voucher. Buy the correct exam next. Do not keep retrying the wrong PIN on WAEC.

## If something fails

- **Nov/Dec vs school candidate:** If the menu offers a private or Nov/Dec option, pick the one that matches the candidate’s registration.
- **Panic buying both:** Buy the one you need. A spare voucher is optional, not a fix.

The exam word on the confirm screen is the product. Everything after that is just payment.
