Ewale does not silently pull money from a wallet. After you confirm the number and amount, the network sends a PIN prompt to the paying number. Until you approve it, airtime has not been bought.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The **paying number** in range, with a working SIM.
2. Your PIN. Nobody on this blog or on Ewale chat will ask for it.
3. A few minutes of patience if the prompt is slow.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Finish the Ewale summary on **\*714\*22#**.
2. Watch for the MoMo / Telecel Cash / AT prompt.
3. Read the amount and merchant name. Approve only if they match.
4. Wait for Ewale’s SMS after a successful PIN.

## After you pay

A declined PIN means zero airtime. A successful PIN means the sale is in motion even if SMS is late.

## If something fails

- **Prompt on a different phone:** The wallet’s registered number gets the PIN, not always the handset you used to dial USSD.
- **Timeout:** Check history. If there is no debit, start a new session. If there is a debit, wait.

The PIN is the payment. The USSD screen is only the order form.
