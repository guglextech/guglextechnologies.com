DSTV on Ewale is a subscription payment against your smartcard, not a token you type on the decoder. After a successful debit, Multichoice restores the bouquet. That can take a few minutes with the decoder on and connected.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Your **DSTV smartcard / decoder number**.
2. A mobile money wallet.
3. The decoder powered if you want to confirm channels after.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Choose **TV Bills** → **DSTV**.
3. Enter the smartcard number. Confirm the account details if shown.
4. Choose the bouquet or amount the menu offers.
5. Approve the PIN. Keep the SMS.

## After you pay

Leave the decoder on. Activation is often 5–15 minutes, not instant like airtime. Do not pay a second month while you wait fifteen minutes.

## Paying for someone else

Enter their smartcard number. Your wallet pays; their decoder renews. Tell them you paid so they do not also pay at a shop.

## If something fails

- **Invalid smartcard:** Remove spaces. Use the number on the card or decoder, not a neighbour’s old screenshot.
- **Channels still off after 30 minutes:** Keep the SMS. Contact Multichoice or Ewale support with the receipt — not a duplicate Ewale payment yet.

Smartcard, bouquet, one PIN, wait for the decoder — not a second debit.
