ECG postpaid is closer to Ghana Water than to prepaid. You are paying an **account**. There is no token to type. Ewale’s **\*714\*22#** is still the collection channel when you have no data.

## Confirm you are postpaid

You receive a **bill** with an outstanding amount. The meter in the house typically has **no** token keypad, or you were never given tokens in SMS. If you have a keypad you use monthly, go back to the prepaid guide — this article will not restore those units.

## Flow on Ewale

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Choose **ECG postpaid** (not prepaid).
3. Enter the **account / customer number** from the bill.
4. Confirm **name** and **outstanding** if shown.
5. Enter the amount — usually the outstanding, unless you are paying a part-payment on purpose.
6. Approve **mobile money**.

No token SMS should be expected. A **receipt / reference** SMS is what you want.

## Reconnection

Paying on Ewale records a collection. **Reconnection** after ECG has cut the line is an ECG field process. Same-day reconnection is not guaranteed because you paid on USSD ten minutes ago.

Carry the reference. Call ECG’s own channels for reconnection timing. Do not buy a prepaid token “to turn it back on” unless you actually have a prepaid meter.

## Part payment

A part payment reduces arrears. It may not stop a scheduled disconnection. If the bill says pay in full, pay in full or speak to ECG — Ewale cannot negotiate the debt.

## Disputes

If you dispute the bill amount, paying via *714*22# still applies money to **that account**. It is not an escrow. Pay disputed amounts only if you intend them to settle the billed debt.

## Proof

Keep:

- Account number.
- Amount, date, time.
- Mobile money debit.
- Ewale reference.

That bundle is what ECG customer service can work with. A description of the USSD menu is not.
