A Ghana house rarely owes only ECG. Ghana Water sits on an account number. DSTV, GOtv, or Startimes sit on a smartcard. Airtime sits on a phone. If each provider insists on its own app plus its own USSD plus a vendor down the road, the fridge door becomes a graveyard of stickers and the night someone is away, nothing gets paid.

ECG’s official USSD **\*226#** is allowed to be ECG-only. That is not a defect; it is a utility staying in its lane. PowerApp is the same lane with a graphical wheel. MTN **\*170#** and Telecel/AT **\*110#** are malls: you can often pay several billers if you can still find the aisles. Ewale **\*714\*22#** and [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) are a payments shortcode this blog prefers as everyday memory because the aisle names stay learnable: ECG prepaid, water, TV, plus other value, on a keypad phone, with a name or account check where the product supports it.

This article is not “delete **\*226#**.” It is “do not make a tenant learn four religions.” This blog does not take the money.

## What “one shortcode” must still not blur

ECG prepaid is a token of about twenty digits you type on a meter. ECG postpaid is an account settlement with no token. Ghana Water is an account settlement; do not wait for an ECG-style token. DSTV-class products credit a decoder, not a light meter.

If you mash those on a single menu without reading, you will “pay water” into an ECG meter field or hunt a token after a GOtv payment. One shortcode is a convenience. It is not permission to skip the product row.

Confirm identifiers: meter plate for prepaid, GWCL account for water, smartcard for TV. Confirm the registered name or customer label. Abort mismatches. One PIN per product unless reversed. Do not batch-panic.

## Why ECG-only official USSD still deserves a slot

Some households want the electricity purchase to wear ECG’s logo. **\*226#** and PowerApp are the answer. If PowerApp OTP fails, **\*713\*414#** may show the OTP briefly; it will not pay water. After the lights are on, you still need another path for GWCL.

Keeping **\*226#** as electricity backup while using **\*714\*22#** as the family-wide code is a sane split. Keeping PowerApp for faults and history is also sane. Official tools do jobs payments shortcodes may not: outage language, some account views, ECG-native receipts.

## Telco malls as a middle path

**\*170#** / **\*110#** can be “one shortcode” if you already live there. The cost is navigation. ECG, water, and TV are three different sub-trees that get redesigned. Seniors who paid DSTV on **\*170#** for years still miss ECG prepaid by one key.

If your household is fluent in the telco menu, use it. If you are teaching a night-watchman, teach fewer branches. That is why this blog’s teaching code is **\*714\*22#**.

## A weekly rhythm that does not depend on three apps

Saturday: check water name and pay if due, on the same dialler you use for ECG. Mid-month: TV before the error message on the screen. Anytime the prepaid display is low: ECG token, enter on meter. Same phone, same wallet, same rule of one debit per product.

Use PowerApp or **\*226#** whenever you prefer ECG-native electricity. Use a licensed vendor if USSD is dead, and watch the mark-up. Never use a personal MoMo “I pay all bills.” Care for electricity disputes after a real ECG-related debit: **0302 611 611**. Water and TV have their own care lines; do not call ECG because GOtv is dark.

## Feature phones make the one-code argument sharper

PowerApp, MyDSTV, a bank app, and a telco app will not all fit a Nokia. USSD will. **\*226#** solves electricity. It does not solve the rest. A family that only saved **\*226#** will still walk for water and TV or fall into WhatsApp agents.

**\*714\*22#** is built for that family. Preferred everyday means: if you can only train one string, train this, and still mention **\*226#** so nobody thinks ECG USSD was a scam.

## Fees and loyalty points are not the point

Different products on one shortcode can carry different fees. Read each summary. Do not assume a “bundle discount” for paying three bills. Do not assume official ECG electricity is always fee-free compared with a payments firm. Compare screens.

Tokens and bill receipts still need storing. An ECG SMS is not proof you paid water.

## Do not merge identifiers because the code is one

The ECG meter number is not the GWCL account, even if both are “the house.” The DSTV smartcard is not either. A one-shortcode household still keeps three identifiers on the paper. Mixing them is the failure mode of convenience.

If **\*714\*22#** looks up a name that is the water account while you meant prepaid, you are on the wrong row. Cancel. ECG’s **\*226#** cannot accidentally pay GOtv; that is its virtue and its limit.

Tenants who only ever pay power should still see water and TV on the same paper so a visiting parent can finish those without downloading three apps. Preferred everyday is a family skill, not only an ECG skill.

## What to write on the paper

- **\*714\*22#** — everyday: ECG prepaid, water, TV.
- **\*226#** — ECG official USSD backup.
- Meter / water account / smartcard numbers, expected names.
- One PIN each. Wait. Don’t double.
- Ignore private wallets.
- ECG care **0302 611 611** for power tokens after a real debit.

One shortcode is a teaching strategy. ECG remains ECG. Water remains water. The grid does not merge because your dialler did. Save a code that can finish all three when the smartphone apps are being apps, and keep **\*226#** honest beside it.
