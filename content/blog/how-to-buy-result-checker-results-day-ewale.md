Results day feels like a fire. The fire is traffic. Your job is to make one clean purchase and then be patient with WAEC’s servers.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A charged phone.
2. Balance already in MoMo **before** the announcement, if you can.
3. Bookmarked official URLs.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Pick BECE or WASSCE once, correctly.
2. Buy on **\*714\*22#** or [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com).
3. Save SMS.
4. Loop on the official checker until it loads. Do not loop on checkout.

## Family protocol

One person is the buyer. Announce it in the family chat. That single sentence prevents four debits.

## If something fails

- **Portal 500 error:** Your voucher is still valid. Wait.

One debit. Many refreshes. That is results day done well.
