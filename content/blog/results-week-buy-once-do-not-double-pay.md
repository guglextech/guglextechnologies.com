On results morning, five relatives will offer to “just buy it.” Ewale will happily sell five vouchers. WAEC will not merge them into one super PIN.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. One person appointed to buy.
2. A five-minute wait after the debit before anyone else dials.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Announce in the family chat that you are paying now.
2. Complete **\*714\*22#** once.
3. Share the SMS. Only then should anyone else spend money.

## After you pay

If SMS is slow, check MoMo history. A successful debit means a voucher is being issued. A second debit is a second voucher.

## If something fails

- **Shop and USSD at the same time:** Pick one channel. Parallel buying is how you pay twice.
- **Failed PIN then success:** The declined attempt did nothing. The later success is the real voucher. Do not add a third.

Appoint one buyer. Wait for one SMS. That is the results-week protocol.
