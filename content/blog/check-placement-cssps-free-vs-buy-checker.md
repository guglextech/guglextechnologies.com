Two tabs: grades and school. Paying for the wrong tab is the most expensive mix-up in September.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. To know which question you are asking.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. “What school?” → [cssps.gov.gh](https://www.cssps.gov.gh/) (free in recent cycles).
2. “What grades?” → BECE voucher on **\*714\*22#** → WAEC.
3. Do not type serial into CSSPS.
4. Do not type date of birth into Ewale.

## After you pay

Unplaced candidates follow GES self-placement instructions on CSSPS, not a second Ewale PIN.

## If something fails

- **Sold a CSSPS checker at a kiosk this year:** Verify against cssps.gov.gh. GES has said checking placement is free.

Free portal for school. Paid voucher for grades. Keep the receipts in the right pocket.
