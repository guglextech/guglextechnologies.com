Results week is a queue of rumours. A simple order saves money: identity, voucher, grades, placement.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Index number.
2. One paying adult.
3. Official URLs saved.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Write the index on paper.
2. Buy **one** BECE checker on **\*714\*22#**.
3. Check grades on the WAEC BECE portal.
4. Later, check [cssps.gov.gh](https://www.cssps.gov.gh/) for the school.

## Rumour control

Nobody can upgrade BECE for a fee. Extra Ewale purchases only make sense for extra candidates or a voucher that never arrived.

## If something fails

- **Family group chat bought four times:** Stop the chat. Count SMS. Use what you have.

Sequence beats speed. One debit per child unless SMS truly failed.
