Printing is not a second purchase. If you already have a working check on screen, use the phone or a café printer. Do not buy another checker “for the printout”.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A successful result page.
2. A printer, or PDF, or a café that accepts a screenshot.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Check on [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/) with the Ewale voucher.
2. Use browser Print / Share → PDF.
3. If you need paper, send the file to a shop. You already paid WAEC access.

## After you pay

Some cafés sell checkers as a bundle with print. If you already bought on Ewale, pay only for paper.

## If something fails

- **Café operator kept your PIN:** Change nothing — the voucher may now be used. Check yourself next time.

One voucher can be enough to see and print. Extra fees should be for ink, not a second PIN.
