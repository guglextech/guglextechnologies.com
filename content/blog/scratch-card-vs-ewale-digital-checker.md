The old roadside card and the Ewale voucher are the same class of product: paid access to a result. The difference is delivery and how you store the secret.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A choice of channel: shop card or **\*714\*22#**.
2. A place to store serial and PIN.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. If you want digital, dial Ewale and buy once.
2. If you already scratched a card, use that card first.
3. Only buy the other format if the first voucher is proven unusable.

## After you pay

Two working vouchers are two fees. That is fine for two candidates. It is waste for one candidate with one index number unless WAEC requires multiple checks.

## If something fails

- **Illegible scratch:** A bad physical card is a shop problem. A missing SMS is an Ewale support problem. Do not mix the complaints.
- **Buying digital because the card “might be fake”:** Try the card on WAEC first if you already paid for it.

Pick one working voucher. Finish the check. Spend more money only for a real second need.
