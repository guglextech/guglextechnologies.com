You can check WASSCE on a phone if you have data **for WAEC’s site**. Buying the checker on USSD did not need data. Viewing grades does.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Ewale SMS with serial and PIN.
2. Index number.
3. Mobile data or Wi-Fi.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Buy on **\*714\*22#** if you do not already have a voucher.
2. Open [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/) in the phone browser.
3. Index number → WASSCE → year → serial → PIN → Submit.
4. Screenshot the result immediately.

## After you pay

If the page spins, wait. Reloading with a new voucher is how people double-pay.

## If something fails

- **No data after buying:** The voucher is already yours. Find Wi-Fi. Do not buy again.

USSD to buy. Browser to look. Two apps, one voucher.
