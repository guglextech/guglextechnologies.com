If you remember one habit from this blog, remember it: read the exam name next to the cedis.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Knowledge of the candidate’s actual exam.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Open the voucher list slowly.
3. If the child is in JHS, **BECE**. If SHS or Nov-Dec, **WASSCE**.
4. Abort with cancel if the label is wrong. Then start again.

## After you pay

A wrong voucher is a new purchase of the right one, plus a leftover PIN you might use for someone on the correct exam.

## If something fails

- **Guessed because the queue was noisy:** Step outside. Read. Then pay.

Label first. PIN second. WAEC will not translate for you.
