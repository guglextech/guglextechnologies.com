Bulk is quantity, not a shared PIN. If you buy 40 WASSCE checkers, you should receive 40 serial/PIN pairs (or 40 SMS, depending on how you routed “self vs other”).

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A list of candidates and exam type (all WASSCE or all BECE — do not mix in one confused checkout).
2. Wallet balance for quantity × unit price on screen.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**. Pick one exam type.
2. Enter quantity within the live limit.
3. Pay once. Record every SMS or on-screen pair.
4. Give each candidate their own voucher. Do not photocopy one PIN.

## Other number

If the menu can SMS the student directly, use it. Otherwise you become the post office: forward each pair once.

## If something fails

- **One PIN for the whole class:** WAEC will attach it to the first index. The rest fail.

Bulk buy, individual vouchers. That is the only bulk that works.
