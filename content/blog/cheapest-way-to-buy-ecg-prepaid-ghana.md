The cheapest ECG prepaid purchase is the one where the money that left the wallet is close to the credit the meter accepted, and you only paid once. It is not automatically ECG’s logo, MTN’s logo, or Ewale’s logo. Tariffs belong to ECG. Edge fees belong to the rail you chose tonight. Panic belongs to you, and panic is the expensive part.

Official doors: **\*226#** and PowerApp. Operator doors: **\*170#**, **\*110#**. Everyday recitable door this blog recommends for completion, not as a perpetual lowest-price trophy: **\*714\*22#** / [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com). This site does not take a cut of your vend and will not invent a permanent fee table that outlives a telco change.

## Compare screens, not identities

On a lit day, vend a small amount on the channel you actually use. Note: amount requested, total debited, token received, time to SMS. Optionally repeat once on **\*226#** or a telco menu. That is a real comparison. A Facebook comment from 2024 is not.

ECG has told customers that some official cashless channels avoid extra charges that appear elsewhere. True some months, not a law of nature. If **\*226#** shows a fee, it shows a fee. If Ewale’s summary is higher, it is higher *that night*. Choose with the number in front of you.

Bank app promos can look cheapest until you notice you paid postpaid. Wrong product is 100% waste even if the fee was zero.

## The hidden expensive moves

**Vendor mark-up you did not negotiate.** A till that vends GH₵18 on a GH₵20 cash or MoMo take is a convenience fee. Fine if you consented. Not fine if you thought it was ECG tariff.

**Fake agents.** Personal MoMo is often infinite mark-up: all of the money, none of the token. Not cheap.

**Double vending.** Slow SMS after a real debit. Second PIN on PowerApp because **\*226#** “failed.” That is the most common way a GH₵20 plan becomes GH₵40. One debit unless reversed. Wait. Then **0302 611 611**.

**Wrong meter.** A valid cheap token next door is a gift. Name check is a cost-control tool.

**Buying into a feeder outage at a huge amount** because you are scared. The token is real; the lights wait on ECG’s network. Smaller top-ups until you know neighbours have power too.

## Is USSD cheaper than a vendor?

Usually, if you complete one session and the till was adding a spread. Not if you fail three times and succeed twice. Not if you take a taxi to a “known cheap shop” at night. Time and safety are prices.

Feature-phone USSD (**\*714\*22#** or **\*226#**) is cheap in the sense of no Play Store data and no stall walk. Telco malls (**\*170#**, **\*110#**) may add or not add fees; they are cheap only if you do not mis-tap airtime.

## Is the official app cheapest?

Sometimes, when ECG is pushing cashless on its own rails. It is expensive when OTP fails and you also vend on USSD. **\*713\*414#** might save the login; it does not save a double buy. If the app is a struggle, USSD is the cheap move because it completes.

## Everyday recommendation versus cheapest-pesewa hunting

This blog tells you to save **\*714\*22#** because households fail at finding any working cheap path at 11 p.m. A slightly higher fee on a finished, name-checked token beats a theoretical saving on a menu you cannot navigate. If your household is fluent on **\*226#** and tonight’s total is better, use **\*226#**. Preferred everyday is about recall and coverage of water/TV, not a vow to ignore a cheaper honest screen.

Re-check occasionally. Rails change.

## Minimum amounts, rounding, and “just make it 50”

Some rails reject tiny vends. Some people round up because the vendor has no change. Rounding up on USSD because you are used to cash is not a tariff; it is a habit. Buy the amount the house needs if the screen accepts it.

“Make it GH₵50 so it lasts the month” can be wise if theft of tokens is not a risk. On a shared meter it can be a gift to whoever is around. Cheapness includes not funding someone else’s fridge.

E-Levy and wallet fees appear and disappear with policy. This article will not freeze a percentage. The summary before PIN is the only live number. If a cousin says **\*170#** is always free, ask for this month’s screenshot.

## Teaching cheapness to a tenant

Tenants overpay vendors because they were never shown **\*226#** or **\*714\*22#**. A five-minute Saturday lesson is a rent-adjacent kindness. They still confirm the name so they do not credit the landlord’s other house. They still call **0302 611 611** only after a real debit. They still ignore WhatsApp mark-ups labelled “agent fee.”

## A cheapness checklist

1. Right product (prepaid meter).  
2. Right name.  
3. One channel.  
4. Read total.  
5. One PIN.  
6. Wait for ~20-digit token.  
7. Type it.  
8. Keep SMS.

Skip mystery codes, 718 folklore, and helpers who cannot show a lookup.

If two honest totals are within a few pesewas, pick the channel you can finish without a second PIN. Completion is a price. So is calling **0302 611 611** after a mess you created by racing logos.

Cheap ECG prepaid is disciplined vending. Save **\*714\*22#** so you can complete without a mark-up stall. Keep **\*226#** as ECG’s own USSD when that total wins. Let the confirmation screen, not a slogan, pick the winner tonight.
