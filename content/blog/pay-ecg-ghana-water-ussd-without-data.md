Utility payments fail most often when the only path is an app. Batteries die mid-KYC. Data bundles finish. Android versions age out. The meter does not care.

Ewale’s **\*714\*22#** is the path that still works on a GSM line: feature phone or smartphone, no data required.

## What USSD needs

- A **SIM** that can place USSD (normal prepaid/postpaid GSM).
- Enough **airtime** is not required for the menu itself; **mobile money** balance is required for the payment.
- The ability to receive a **PIN prompt** and an **SMS**.

If mobile money prompts are blocked on the line (some new SIMs, some unregistered wallets), USSD will open and the payment will still fail at the prompt. That is a wallet problem, not an Ewale menu problem.

## When to use USSD instead of the web

Use **\*714\*22#** when:

- You are at the meter in a basement or peri-urban area with poor data.
- You are paying for a parent who only uses a keypad phone.
- The Ewale site is slow on your current network.
- You need ECG or water paid **now**, not after an app update.

Use the **web** when you need history, a long meter number you can paste, or to retrieve a token from a previous order with a larger screen.

## Keep the menu short in your head

A working utility session is:

1. Service (ECG prepaid / ECG postpaid / Ghana Water).
2. Identifier (meter or account).
3. Confirm name.
4. Amount.
5. PIN prompt.

If you wander into airtime or other Ewale services by mistake, cancel and dial **\*714\*22#** again. Do not confirm an amount on the wrong product.

## Sessions die. Payments might not.

USSD will drop if you pause too long, if the network is busy, or if the phone loses signal. That dropped session is **not** proof the debit failed.

After a drop:

- Check the **prompt**. It may still be on the screen.
- Check **mobile money**.
- Only then decide whether to start a new order.

## SMS is part of the product

For ECG prepaid, the token SMS *is* the delivery. A phone with a full inbox, or an SMS filter, will look like “Ewale didn’t send it.”
