There is no “change destination” button after PIN. Airtime on 024… stays there. Tokens bind to meters. DSTV binds to smartcards. Type slower than you spend.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Source documents: SIM, meter sticker, decoder card.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Copy from the object, not from memory.
2. Use the name preview when Ewale offers it.
3. If you already paid the wrong destination, treat that as done. Pay the right one next if you still need it, and ask support only about recovery — not magic conversion.

## After you pay

Sometimes the “wrong” house is family and you can just tell them. Sometimes it is a stranger. Either way the first sale stands.

## If something fails

- **Transposed digits:** Read back in groups of three or four.
- **Old WhatsApp number:** People port and replace SIMs. Ask for a fresh photo.

The confirm screen is the last cheap step. After PIN, you own that destination.
