The debit is ordinary Mobile Money. The product is a WAEC serial and PIN. If you only watch the USSD menu close, you have not finished — the PIN prompt is the payment.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A funded MoMo wallet on the line you are dialling from.
2. The exam type: **WASSCE** or **BECE**.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#** from the wallet’s number.
2. Pick Results Vouchers / Education.
3. Confirm amount and exam name.
4. Enter the MoMo PIN on the **prompt**, not inside a guessed USSD field.

## After you pay

Wallet SMS plus Ewale voucher SMS. Keep both. One is the debit proof; the other is the checker.

## Which wallet

Ewale accepts the Ghana wallets on the live menu (MTN, Telecel, AT). Use the wallet that actually has the balance. Do not split one checker across two phones.

## If something fails

- **Insufficient funds:** Top up, then buy once. A failed PIN is not a voucher.

MoMo pays Ewale. Ewale delivers the voucher. WAEC still holds the result.
