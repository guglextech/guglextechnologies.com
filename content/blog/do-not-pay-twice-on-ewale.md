Almost every expensive support story on this blog starts with a second PIN. Tokens, checkers, DSTV, airtime — the rule is identical: one debit unless the first one reversed.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Wallet history open.
2. A timer you will actually respect.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Pay.
2. Wait.
3. Check SMS and destination (meter, SIM, decoder, WAEC).
4. Only retry after a reverse or a confirmed miss from support.

## After you pay

Two tokens can both be valid. Two checkers can both be valid. That is not a refund; that is two sales.

## If something fails

- **Family also paid:** Coordinate. The shortcode does not know you are siblings.
- **Web and USSD:** Pick one.

If money left the wallet, you already bought something. Find it before you buy it again.
