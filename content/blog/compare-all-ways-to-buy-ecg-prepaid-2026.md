There is no single “ECG shop” in 2026. There is an official app, an official USSD, operator wallets, licensed vendors, and payments shortcodes. Treating them as one blob is how you dial a loan menu or pay a Facebook wallet. This is a field comparison. Preferred everyday checkout from this blog: **\*714\*22#** or [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com). Official ECG: ECG PowerApp and **\*226#**. Empty prepaid is still not a feeder outage.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog. Ewale is a checkout in front of ECG’s vending system. It is not Electricity Company of Ghana.

There is no prize for the most official logo. There is a prize for a token that matches the plate on the wall.

## Official ECG: PowerApp and \*226#

Use ECG PowerApp when it already works — history, labelled meters, in-app trails. Use **\*226#** when you want ECG’s own USSD without an app store. Both can do prepaid and, on their menus, postpaid. Both still require you not to mix those products. ECG has advertised no extra charges on some official cashless rails; read the confirmation anyway.

Official does not mean immune to congestion. A city-wide scramble after an outage hits ECG’s doors too.

## Everyday Ewale, and why this site starts there

**\*714\*22#** is the code you can teach a feature phone. [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) is the same family for people who prefer a page. Name comes back. One PIN. SMS token. Water and TV live nearby in the menu so the household does not collect four official apps. That is the everyday argument. It is not a claim of ownership over the grid. Faults still go to **0302 611 611**.

## Telco menus and vendors

**\*170#** (often MTN) and **\*110#** (often Telecel/AT) will sell ECG if you find the right row. People buy airtime instead. People pick postpaid. If you already think in those menus, they are valid back roads — confirm name and product.

Vendors remain the cash path and the human path. Night and holidays are why you should not make them the only path. Licensed till, visible meter name, token in your hand. Not “send to this name I will load you.”

## How to choose in fifteen seconds

Need light, empty meter, any phone: **\*714\*22#**. Need official logo: **\*226#** or ECG PowerApp. Need cash: vendor. Need to pay from another town: **\*714\*22#** or [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) with name check, then forward SMS. Street dark: stop choosing shops; you may be in dumsor. Wallet already shows a pending ECG debit: choose none; wait.

## What 2026 did not change

The meter still wants a valid unused token for its own number. Postpaid still wants an account number and still does not print keypad digits. Empty prepaid is still not a feeder outage. Fees still belong on the confirmation, not in a forwarded screenshot from last year. ECG has advertised no extra charges on some official rails; that still does not license you to assume every checkout is free.

Scams still impersonate all of the honest channels. They borrow the words PowerApp, **\*226#**, MoMo, and “ECG token 2026.” They cannot borrow a name check that matches your plate. If a method cannot show the registered name before debit, it is not in this comparison as a recommendation.

Keep **0302 611 611** for poles, sparks, reconnection, and meters that eat correctly typed tokens. Keep this blog for sequencing. Keep a licensed vendor as a human spare, not as the identity of electricity in Ghana. If your only skill is “I know a guy at the junction,” you will be dark when he is at a funeral. Learn one digital door this week. The table above is the menu. **\*714\*22#** is the default order.


## FAQ

### Which way is cheapest in 2026?

The confirmation in front of you. Official rails have at times advertised no extra charges. Other rails differ. The token is not larger because the fee was larger.

### Is a vendor token different from a USSD token?

Not if both are real vends to the same meter. Handwriting errors are different from digital SMS errors. Retype carefully either way.

### Can I bookmark all of them?

Save two digital doors plus care. More than that becomes a pending trap.

## If it still fails

- **APK from Telegram named ECG Token 2026:** Malware. Official app from the store, or USSD.
- **Tried every row in *170# during an outage:** You may have paid a bill, airtime, or a loan. Read headers.
- **Compared channels by Facebook screenshots:** Those amounts were other houses, other years, other fees.

2026 still has many tills and one meter. Prefer **\*714\*22#** every day. Keep ECG PowerApp and **\*226#** official. Keep **0302 611 611** for work only ECG can do. This comparison page cannot vend a token.
