Renewing a day early is calmer than renewing at kickoff. Ewale does not need the box to already be dark. Paying early is still one subscription payment, not a glitch.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. IUC.
2. The package you actually want next, not last year’s assumption.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#** → **TV Bills** → **GOtv**.
2. Confirm IUC and amount.
3. Approve once. Ignore the urge to “test” with a second small payment.

## After you pay

If the box still has days left, the new payment typically extends. You should not need a midnight countdown.

## If something fails

- **Changed package accidentally:** Read the bouquet name on the summary. Support cannot always reshape a completed bouquet pick.
- **Paid DSTV by habit:** Wrong row. Check before PIN.

Early renewals are valid. Double renewals are just two charges.
