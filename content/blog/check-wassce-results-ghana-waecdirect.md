Bookmark the real host: ghana.waecdirect.org. Ads in search results sometimes wrap a clone. Your Ewale PIN should be typed only on WAEC.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A WASSCE voucher (serial + PIN).
2. Index number and exam year.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Purchase on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**) if needed.
2. Go to [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/).
3. Fill every labelled box. Do not paste the Ewale payment reference into PIN.
4. Submit. Print or save PDF if the browser offers it.

## What Ewale cannot do

Ewale cannot “open” ghana.waecdirect.org for you and cannot reset a PIN WAEC already accepted. Support is for missing SMS after a real debit.

## If something fails

- **Certificate request vs checker:** Attestation and certificates are other WAEC services. A result checker only shows the result page.

Official URL, correct year, voucher from a checkout you trust.
