AT (AirtelTigo) airtime is a separate product from MTN and Telecel on Ewale. Picking the wrong network still takes money and credits a number that may not be the one you meant.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The **AT number** you want to credit.
2. A funded mobile money wallet.
3. Time to finish the USSD session before it drops.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Choose **Telco** → **Airtime** → **AT** / AirtelTigo.
3. Enter the number. Confirm the digits come back as you typed them.
4. Enter the amount and approve the PIN.

## After you pay

AT credit is usually instant. Save the SMS. Support needs the reference, not a screenshot of the USSD screen after it closed.

## Glo numbers

Ewale also lists Glo on some airtime menus. Use the Glo row for Glo SIMs. Do not send Glo credit through the AT option.

## If something fails

- **Wrong network selected:** You cannot convert an MTN airtime sale into AT. Buy AT on the AT row.
- **Session dropped:** Look at MoMo history before retrying.

Match the menu to the SIM. The number alone is not enough if you picked the wrong operator.
