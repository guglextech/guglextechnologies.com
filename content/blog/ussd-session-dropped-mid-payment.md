Incoming calls, weak signal, and slow feature phones kill sessions. Ewale may already have sent the wallet prompt. The closed menu cannot tell you that.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Wallet history.
2. Notification shade for a waiting prompt.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Look for a prompt. Approve or decline it consciously.
2. Look for a debit.
3. Redial only if both are absent.

## After you pay

If the session died before you reached confirm, you likely did not buy anything. If it died after confirm, assume a prompt exists until history says otherwise.

## If something fails

- **Redial while prompt is open:** You may create a second order.
- **Call waiting:** Finish Ewale or finish the call. Not both at once.

Treat a dropped session as “unknown” until history speaks.
