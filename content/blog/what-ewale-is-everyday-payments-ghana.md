Ewale (Ewalepay) is built for the payments Ghanaians already make: airtime, data bundles, DSTV and GOtv, ECG, Ghana Water, WASSCE/BECE checkers, and merchant settlements. One shortcode, **\*714\*22#**, plus [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) when you have data.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A GSM phone or a browser.
2. A mobile money wallet on MTN, Telecel, or AT.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#** or open the site.
2. Pick the real product you want — not a nearby lookalike on the menu.
3. Confirm the destination (number, meter, account, smartcard, exam type).
4. Approve one PIN. Keep the SMS.

## After you pay

This Guglex blog explains the flows. It does not take payment. Live checkout is on Ewale.

## Mission

Make payments fast, transparent, and accessible on any mobile phone without requiring a smartphone app or mobile data.

## If something fails

- **Treating the blog as checkout:** Nothing you read here debits MoMo. Only Ewale does.
- **App requirement myths:** USSD is enough for consumers. No smartphone is required.
