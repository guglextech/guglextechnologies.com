The exam does not care which wallet paid. WAEC will not see Telecel or MTN. They will see serial and PIN.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Telecel Cash balance for the on-screen amount.
2. The correct exam row.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. From the Telecel line, dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. BECE or WASSCE / Nov-Dec.
3. Approve Cash PIN.
4. Read SMS on that same SIM.

## After you pay

If you expected the SMS on MTN, you bought on the wrong handset.

## If something fails

- **Dual SIM paid Telecel, watching MTN inbox:** Open the Telecel inbox.

Wallet is a pipe. Exam type is the product.
