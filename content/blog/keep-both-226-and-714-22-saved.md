One code in your head is a single point of failure dressed as simplicity. **\*226#** is Electricity Company of Ghana’s official USSD. **\*714\*22#** is the everyday Ewale shortcode this site wants you to actually remember. Save both. Use Ewale first on a normal night. Use **\*226#** when you want ECG’s own menu or when the everyday door is busy. Use ECG PowerApp when the smartphone is already logged in.

Do not save a stranger’s MoMo as the third code. Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog. Ewale is a checkout in front of ECG’s vending system. It is not Electricity Company of Ghana.

## What to write on the paper

Three lines. “Everyday: **\*714\*22#** — Ewale, confirm name, ECG prepaid.” “ECG official USSD: **\*226#**.” “ECG voice: 0302 611 611.” Optional fourth: “PowerApp on the Android in the drawer.” Optional fifth: the meter number labelled prepaid, or the account number labelled postpaid. If you have both products in one family, write both identifiers with words, not two similar digit strings.

Paper beats a contact named “power” that someone later overwrites with a vendor’s personal number.

## When to reach for which code

Normal empty meter, any GSM phone: **\*714\*22#**. You also want water or TV in the same session of life: **\*714\*22#**. Relative who will only trust ECG branding: **\*226#** or ECG PowerApp. PowerApp OTP missing: skip the app, do not skip electricity — **\*714\*22#** then **\*226#**. Telco bill-pay (**\*170#**, **\*110#**) is a back road if you already live in those menus; it is easier to pick the wrong ECG product there.

If the street is off, neither code relights the feeder. Buy only if you were empty. Report faults on **0302 611 611**.

## Session drops and how two codes save you

USSD dies. You do not know if you paid. That is a wallet-history moment, not a “dial the other code immediately” moment. If nothing left the wallet, the other code is a clean retry. If a debit exists, stay with that checkout’s support and SMS inbox. Switching from **\*714\*22#** to **\*226#** to “force a token” is how pending becomes two tokens.

Two saved codes are for availability, not for simultaneous bets.

## Teach the house the difference

A teenager who thinks **\*226#** is “the old code” will not use it when Ewale is congested. A parent who thinks **\*714\*22#** is ECG will argue with a care agent. Ten sentences in daylight prevent both. Official versus everyday. Prepaid token versus postpaid account. Empty versus dumsor.

Keep [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) on the same paper for people who hate USSD steps and have data. Web and **\*714\*22#** are the same checkout family, not a third utility.

## What not to save

Random \*718\* rumours. PowerApp OTP viewer codes labelled as “buy power.” WhatsApp “agent” shortcodes. Last tenant’s vendor. A loan menu that lives near the same digits in muscle memory. Read the header every time you dial, even when you are sure.

## Practice both codes before you need them

A code you have never dialled will fail you in the dark even if it is written in marker. On a calm afternoon, open **\*226#** far enough to see the ECG header — you do not have to pay. Then open **\*714\*22#** far enough to see Ewale and the ECG prepaid row. Confirm that the meter number you wrote still returns the name you expect. A GH₵10 test token is optional; a name check is not.

Show the teenager the difference in the headers. Show the parent that **\*714\*22#** is not the care line. Show everyone that hanging up before PIN is not a purchase, and that a debit in MoMo history is. The paper on the fridge is a map. The practice is the driving lesson.

If ECG PowerApp is installed, add the prepaid meter there too, still labelled prepaid. You now have three doors and one identifier. That is resilience. Three doors used in the same pending hour is still a mistake. The saved pair of shortcodes exists so that when OTP dies, or when Ewale is congested, you do not invent a fourth door from Facebook.

Keep the paper out of the steam of the kettle and out of the child’s crayon reach. A faded **\*226#** that looks like another number is how people wander into menus they cannot explain. Rewrite it when it fades. The grid will still be there. Your memory of digits will not.


## FAQ

### If I only remember one, which should it be?

This site says **\*714\*22#** for everyday. Then add **\*226#** as soon as you have another strip of tape. Official ECG without a backup app story is **\*226#**.

### Does saving both confuse the meter?

No. The meter never sees the shortcode. It sees the token.

### Should I save *170# as well?

**\*170#** can reach ECG billers on MTN. It is optional. It is easier to mis-tap. Two electricity-focused codes plus care is enough for most houses.

## If it still fails

- **Dialled the care number as USSD:** 0302 611 611 is voice. **\*226#** and **\*714\*22#** are USSD.
- **Used both codes in one pending payment:** Wallet first. Then one support path.
- **Wrote only the numbers, no labels:** Next tenant will mix prepaid and postpaid. Label the product.

Two codes on paper beat one code in a panic. Everyday: **\*714\*22#**. Official USSD: **\*226#**. Official app: ECG PowerApp. Voice: **0302 611 611**. This blog is none of those tills.
