Agents are useful. They are not mandatory. If you already have MoMo float, Ewale lets you skip the queue, the “network is off” stall, and the after-dark closed shutter.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Wallet float.
2. The correct destination number or meter.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Skip the shop if you only needed them as a middleman.
2. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
3. Pay the actual biller product, not “give the agent cash to try.”

## After you pay

If an agent already took cash to pay for you, do not also pay on Ewale for the same bill until you know the agent failed.

## If something fails

- **Cash to agent plus USSD:** That is two payment attempts. Reconcile with the agent first.
- **Agent used wrong meter:** Your own USSD with a confirmed name is the safer path next time.

When you can dial, you can pay. Keep agents for cash-in, not as a required extra hop.
