Ghana Water payments are **account payments**, not tokens. You are paying against a customer account. There is no code to type into a meter. The proof of payment is the successful collection — and, later, the balance on your bill.

Ewale collects Ghana Water on **\*714\*22#** so you can pay without an app and without data.

## What you need

- Your **Ghana Water account number** (from a bill, SMS, or the customer service centre).
- Enough **mobile money** to cover the amount you intend to pay.
- Time: the payment can succeed immediately while the **utility ledger** updates later.

If you only have an old bill, use the account number printed on it. Names on the bill should match the lookup on Ewale before you confirm.

## Steps on *714*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Choose **Ghana Water** (or water / GWCL, depending on the menu label).
3. Enter the **account number**.
4. Confirm the **name** and, where shown, the **outstanding balance**.
5. Enter the **amount** you want to pay.
6. Confirm.
7. Approve the **mobile money PIN prompt**.

Pay at least the outstanding amount if you want the bill cleared. Partial payments are possible; they reduce the balance, they do not close the account.

## Why the bill can still look unpaid

Ghana Water posts many payments in **batches**. Ewale can have a successful payment while the printed or online bill still shows the old balance for hours — sometimes until the next posting cycle.

That delay is not a failed payment. Check:

- Mobile money **debit** on your phone.
- Ewale **confirmation** SMS, if sent.
- The **reference** in that message.

If those exist, the money left your wallet. The remaining wait is the utility updating its own ledger. A second payment in that window is a second payment, not a retry.

## How much to pay

- **Outstanding balance:** use the figure from the lookup, not from memory.
- **Overpayment:** usually sits as credit on the account. Confirm with Ghana Water if you intend to overpay by a large amount.
- **Underpayment:** the account stays in arrears. Disconnection risk does not disappear because you paid something.

## After you pay

Keep the reference. If a customer service desk asks for proof, the mobile money statement plus the Ewale reference is what they need — not a screenshot of a USSD session that already closed.

If you pay for a compound or a landlord’s account, confirm the account number with them in writing before you dial. Water accounts are easy to mix up in shared houses.
