The old pattern was travel, queue, cash. Ewale’s pattern is a photo of the smartcard and a PIN. The decoder does not care that you are in another region.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A photo of the card / IUC.
2. Network signal for USSD.
3. Someone at home to power the decoder afterwards.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Get the photo.
2. Pay the matching TV row on **\*714\*22#**.
3. Tell the house to leave the box on.

## After you pay

If they still travel to town “to be sure,” they may pay again. Send the SMS so the trip is unnecessary.

## If something fails

- **Blurry photo:** Ask for another picture. Do not guess the last digit.
- **Decoder off for hours:** Activation may sit until the box is alive. That is not always a failed payment.

Transport money is not a TV bill. USSD is.
