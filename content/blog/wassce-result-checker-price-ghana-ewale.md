People search “WASSCE checker price” because shops shout different numbers. On Ewale you pay what the summary shows for the WASSCE / Nov-Dec voucher (commonly GH₵24 per voucher on the live menu — always read the screen).

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Wallet balance at least the **on-screen** total, including quantity.
2. WASSCE selected so you are not quoting a BECE row.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#** and open the WASSCE checker row.
2. Set quantity. Read **GH₵** total.
3. If the total is wrong, abort. Do not approve a surprise figure.
4. Approve once.

## After you pay

Price buys access, not an A in Maths. Nobody legitimate sells upgraded results.

## FAQ

### Does a higher price mean more checks?

No. Quantity means more vouchers. One voucher is typically reusable a few times for the **same** index and year on WAEC’s rules — not for a second person.

### Is web cheaper than USSD?

Use the amount on that checkout. Do not assume a discount because you opened a browser.

## If something fails

- **Agent added “service charge”:** If you already paid them, that is between you and the agent. Buying on Ewale yourself shows one total.

The honest price is the one on the Ewale summary before the PIN.
