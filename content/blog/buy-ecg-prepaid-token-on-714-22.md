ECG prepaid is not wallet credit in an app. After settlement, Ewale sends a token. That string of digits is the product. Until you enter it on the correct meter, the house stays dark.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The **meter number** on the meter body.
2. A mobile money wallet with enough balance.
3. The phone that receives the PIN and the token SMS.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Choose **ECG** / prepaid electricity.
3. Enter the meter number. Confirm the registered name. If it is not your household, stop.
4. Enter the amount in cedis. Approve the PIN.
5. Wait for the token SMS. Type it on the meter keypad.

## After you pay

A successful debit without an immediate token is not a reason to buy a second token. Wait, then recover the first one.

## If something fails

- **Wrong house:** A valid token for someone else’s meter cannot be redirected.
- **Meter rejected token:** Retype carefully. Use the latest SMS. Confirm you are at the meter you paid for.

One confirmed debit, one token. The SMS is the receipt and the product.
