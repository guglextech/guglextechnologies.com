Empty states have several authors: WAEC release timing, your typos, or a voucher for the wrong exam. Only one of those is Ewale’s SMS.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The SMS.
2. A second pair of eyes on the index.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Confirm WAEC has released that exam.
2. Confirm School vs Private.
3. Confirm year and index.
4. If the PIN itself is rejected after all that, then investigate the voucher with Ewale **if it never worked and SMS looks incomplete**.

## After you pay

Withheld or cancelled is a WAEC status. More MoMo will not clear it.

## If something fails

- **Released tomorrow, bought three today:** Keep one voucher. Wait for the release notice.

Diagnose the portal before you diagnose the payment.
