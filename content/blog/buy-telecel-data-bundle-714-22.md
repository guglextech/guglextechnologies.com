Telecel data on Ewale is a named bundle, not a custom gigabyte slider. The menu lists packs. The one you confirm is the one that attaches to the number.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A **Telecel number**.
2. Clarity on whether you want a small daily pack or a monthly pack.
3. Enough mobile money for the price shown, not a guess from last month.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Open **Data bundles** and select **Telecel**.
3. Enter the number and choose the pack.
4. Approve one prompt. Stay on the line until Ewale says it was sent.

## After you pay

If the pack is for a child’s SIM, tell them not to buy another pack from a vendor until your SMS shows success.

## If something fails

- **Pack names look similar:** Read validity, not only the price. A cheaper pack may expire tomorrow.
- **Debit, no data:** Wait, check Telecel bundle balance, then support with the SMS reference.

One debit, one pack. The SMS is the receipt for the bundle you actually selected.
