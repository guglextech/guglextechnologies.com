Ewale runs through weekends and holidays because bills do too. Support is for missing tokens, missing vouchers, and pending debits — not for sharing PINs or converting a wrong product into a different one.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Date, amount, destination identifier, and **reference**.
2. The paying number.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Try SMS and wallet history first so you do not open a ticket for a message you missed.
2. Dial **\*714\*22#** → **Support**, or email **walletewale@gmail.com**.
3. Paste the reference. Never paste your PIN.

## After you pay

Wrong-product purchases (BECE instead of WASSCE, prepaid instead of postpaid) usually cannot be “changed.” You may need a new, correct payment.

## If something fails

- **WhatsApp strangers:** Official channels first. PIN collectors hide in comment sections.
- **No reference:** MoMo history still has one. Copy it.

Reference in, PIN never. That is how you get help without creating a second problem.
