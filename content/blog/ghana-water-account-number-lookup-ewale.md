Ghana Water does not take a meter keypad token. It takes an **account**. If the account is wrong, you have funded someone else’s bill.

## Where the number lives

- The **paper or PDF bill**: account / customer number, not the amount due.
- SMS from Ghana Water, if you are on their alerts.
- A previous **Ewale** confirmation (if you saved it).
- The district customer service desk, with ID and the service address.

Do not use a neighbour’s number “because we share the standpipe” unless that is the legal account. Shared taps often still sit on **one** billed account.

## Lookup on *714*22#

Dial **\*714\*22#**, choose Ghana Water, enter the account number. You should see a **name**. Compare it to the bill.

If the name is a company or a previous tenant, that can still be the live account — utilities lag on name changes. Confirm with the person who actually receives disconnection notices.

If lookup fails, you do not have a payable account on the file Ewale is using. Fix the number before you invent an amount.

## Outstanding balance

Where Ewale shows a balance, treat it as **indicative**. It can be a few hours stale relative to a payment you made this morning, or relative to a bill printed last week.

Use it to choose an amount, not as a legal statement of debt. If you are in a dispute with Ghana Water about a leaked pipe or an estimated bill, paying on Ewale still pays **that account**. It does not freeze the dispute.

## Compounds and landlords

Pay the account whose name you confirmed. If tenants contribute, collect their cash first or pay and record it — Ewale will not split one debit into five tenant receipts.

Landlords who want tenants to pay directly should give them:

- The exact account number.
- The name that must appear on lookup.
- The amount this month.

Then tenants use **\*714\*22#** themselves, from their own mobile money, so each person has a reference.

## After payment

Save the reference against the month. “I paid in September” without a reference is not useful in October when the next bill ignores a payment that was still in a batch.

The account number is the identity. The reference is the proof. You need both.
