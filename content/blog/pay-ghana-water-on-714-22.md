Ghana Water is a billed account, similar to ECG postpaid. There is no meter token. A successful Ewale debit can still take hours to display on the water bill.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The **GWCL account number**.
2. The name you expect to see when Ewale looks it up.
3. Wallet cover for the amount plus any fee shown.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Choose **Ghana Water** / water bill.
3. Enter the account. Confirm the name. Stop if it is the wrong house.
4. Enter the amount. Approve the PIN.

## After you pay

Keep the SMS even if the next bill still looks old for a while. A second payment while the first is posting is a second payment.

## If something fails

- **Wrong account:** You paid that account. Paying yours afterwards is extra, not a reversal.
- **No token SMS:** Water should not send an ECG-style token. Look for a payment confirmation instead.

Confirm the name. Pay once. Use the SMS if the bill is slow to move.
