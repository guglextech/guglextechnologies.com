If the child already knows the grades from school and you only want the SHS name, start at cssps.gov.gh. If you have no grades yet, buy a BECE checker on Ewale, then place later.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A clear goal: grades, placement, or both.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Grades: **\*714\*22#** → BECE voucher → [eresults.waecgh.org](https://eresults.waecgh.org/).
2. Placement: [cssps.gov.gh](https://www.cssps.gov.gh/) with index and date of birth.
3. Self-placement, if offered, is still on CSSPS — not on Ewale.

## After you pay

Buying two BECE checkers will not change the school CSSPS gave you.

## If something fails

- **Paid for “placement upgrade”:** That is not a WAEC checker. Stop.

Name the job: grades or school. Pay only for the job that needs a voucher.
