The portal is a form. Treat it like a bank transfer: every box has one job.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. WASSCE voucher SMS.
2. Index and year.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Visit [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/) only.
2. Examination type: WASSCE School or Private.
3. Year: the sitting, not “this year” by reflex.
4. Serial, then PIN from Ewale. Submit.

## Reference numbers

Ewale’s payment reference is for support tickets. It is not a WAEC PIN.

## If something fails

- **HTTPS warning / odd subdomain:** Leave. Type the host yourself.

Form on WAEC. Receipt on Ewale. Do not mix the numbers.
