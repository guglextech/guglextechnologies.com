Search ads invent “WAEC checker login” pages. The official hosts are short and dull. Bookmark them before results week.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The exam you sat.
2. A voucher from a real checkout, not from the fake page.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. WASSCE → [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/).
2. BECE → [eresults.waecgh.org](https://eresults.waecgh.org/).
3. Buy the matching voucher on **\*714\*22#**.
4. If the site asks for extra “activation fees”, leave.

## waecgh.org

WAEC Ghana also publishes notices on waecgh.org. Follow links from there if you are unsure. Do not follow sponsored “result now” buttons.

## If something fails

- **Portal asked for your MoMo PIN:** WAEC does not take your Ewale PIN. You already paid Ewale.

Find the portal once. Save it. Buy the voucher on Ewale, not on a banner.
