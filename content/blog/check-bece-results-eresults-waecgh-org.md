eresults.waecgh.org is a WAEC Ghana results host. Use it when WAEC points BECE checks there. If WAEC publishes a different BECE URL that year, use that one — not a Facebook clone.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. BECE serial and PIN from Ewale.
2. Index number entered twice if the form asks you to confirm.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Buy BECE on **\*714\*22#**.
2. Open [eresults.waecgh.org](https://eresults.waecgh.org/).
3. Select BECE (School) or BECE (Private).
4. Year, serial, PIN, captcha if shown. Submit once.

## After you pay

Download or screenshot. Printing can wait. Losing the only view of the page is worse.

## If something fails

- **Used a WASSCE PIN here:** Wrong exam type. Buy BECE.

Match portal, exam type, and voucher. Then stop buying.
