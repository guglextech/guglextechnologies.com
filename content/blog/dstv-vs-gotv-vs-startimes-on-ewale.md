The Ewale TV menu is a list of companies. Paying the wrong company with a number that happens to fit the field is how money disappears into a valid sale you did not mean.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The brand printed on the decoder.
2. That brand’s number (smartcard or IUC).

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Look at the box. Read the logo.
2. Open that exact row under **TV Bills**.
3. Enter that exact number. Confirm. Pay once.

## After you pay

You can pay all three houses in one evening. Do them as three sessions, three SMS files, not one confused session.

## If something fails

- **Compound with mixed decoders:** Label the photos: “Maame DSTV”, “shop GOtv”.
- **Same amount, different brand:** Amount matching last month is not proof you picked the right biller.

Logo → menu row → number → PIN. Skip any arrow and you are gambling.
