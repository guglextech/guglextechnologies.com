Ewale is a payments product. A WAEC result checker is one of the education items on the menu. Serial and PIN come by SMS after a successful debit.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The exam name.
2. A wallet PIN you will not share in chat.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Results Vouchers: BECE or WASSCE / Nov-Dec.
3. Approve the amount on screen.
4. Store the SMS.

## FAQ

### Where do I pay?

ewalepay.com or *714*22#. Not in the comments of this article.

### Support?

Use the support option on the USSD menu or email walletewale@gmail.com with the debit reference. Nobody from Ewale needs your MoMo PIN.

### Bulk?

The menu lets you set quantity (up to the live limit, often dozens per transaction) for a class or PTA.

## If something fails

- **Phishing “Ewale agent”:** Hang up. Real checkout is the shortcode or the website.
