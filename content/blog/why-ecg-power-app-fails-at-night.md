ECG PowerApp has a reputation for dying after 9pm. Some of that reputation is ECG’s own servers under a national habit: people buy prepaid when the LCD finally looks scary, which is after dinner. Some of it is not ECG at all. It is the compound radio, the OTP SMS, Play Services, and a phone on 15 percent battery.

Understanding the mix stops you from paying three times before midnight. This blog does not process those payments. Official ECG USSD (**\*226#**) is built for the no-data half of the story. **\*714\*22#** is the shortcode this site wants on the fridge because night users will not navigate a four-level official menu while children shout.

## Night is a traffic story

When a region’s results-week-style surge hits electricity instead of WAEC, vending queues lengthen. HTTPS APIs time out. The app shows a white screen or “try again.” The same minute, USSD sessions also drop — but USSD failures are obvious (the session dies) while app failures look like you should tap again.

Tap again is the night tax. If the first tap already created a debit, the second tap is a second token. Check MoMo in the poor light. If the wallet moved, sit down. Search SMS for twenty digits. Enter them. Sleep.

If the wallet did not move, one more attempt on a **different radio** is smarter than ten attempts on PowerApp. That different radio is **\*226#** or **\*714\*22#**.

## Night is an SMS story

OTP for PowerApp login is an SMS. Token delivery is an SMS. At night, SMS centres delay. ECG documents **\*713\*414#** as a way to read a fresh OTP if you act quickly. That shortcode is not a token shop. If you are past the OTP window, you are not past electricity. You are past this login.

Token SMS can arrive ten minutes late while you are already in a taxi to a 24-hour vendor. Wait on a known debit. Vendors at night charge convenience. They cannot retrieve a token that is about to hit your inbox.

Dual-SIM at night: the “data SIM” is the one with the night bundle; the “SMS SIM” is the ECG login. The OTP went to the quiet SIM. People then enable data on the login SIM, kill the bundle, and still have no OTP because they already burned the window. Put the wallet SIM in the slot that receives SMS, or use USSD from that SIM directly.

## Night is a power-and-radio story

When the prepaid meter dies, the router dies. PowerApp needed the router. The phone falls back to a weak 4G that worked for WhatsApp status but not for ECG’s payment handshake. USSD still might work at the compound gate. Pay at the gate. Walk back. Type the token.

Low battery plus a bright PowerApp theme plus a spinning loader is how phones shut down after PIN and before receipt. If you are at 10 percent, use USSD. It is faster. Plug the phone into a neighbour’s socket after the token, not before the PIN.

### Why vendors look attractive after 10pm

The booth has a light (sometimes). The human takes cash or MoMo and types for you. ECG has pushed cashless vending, so even the booth may demand MoMo. You have not escaped the radio; you have added a queue.

If you can stand in that queue, you can stand at the gate and dial **\*714\*22#**. Confirm the name. One PIN. SMS. The vendor is a backup for when **all** electronic channels fail, or for card meters you cannot write at home.

## A night playbook that is not heroic

1. Look at the LCD. Empty prepaid is not an outage on the feeder. If the whole street is dark, buying a token will not light the transformer. Call **0302 611 611** for a feeder fault; do not MoMo.
2. If it is your meter, open PowerApp **once** if you already have data and a saved meter.
3. If OTP or loaders appear, stop. **\*713\*414#** only for a fresh OTP. Otherwise USSD.
4. Confirm name. Pay once. Wait for the token. Enter it. Do not open a second app for courage.

[ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) at night is fine if you still have data (phone hotspot from a neighbour, a shop’s Wi-Fi). It is the wrong tool if the night’s problem is the radio.

## Morning-after cleanup

If you panic-bought twice, enter tokens in order, keep both receipts, and do not throw the unused SMS away. If one payment pending-reversed into the PowerApp wallet, spend it later; do not tell yourself ECG stole the night.

If you never got in, you did not need to “fix” PowerApp at 2am. Fix it on Saturday: update the app, test OTP, save the meter, write **\*714\*22#** larger on the paper.

## FAQ

### Is ECG’s vending actually closed at night?

Prepaid electronic vending is designed to be always-on. Slowness is not a posted closing time. A local outage of systems can still happen. Treat a confirmed debit as a purchase in a queue, not as permission to use a second brand.

### Why does \*226# also fail at midnight sometimes?

Same national habit, plus USSD congestion. Try once more after a minute, or try **\*714\*22#**. If both fail, wait. Walking to a vendor is valid. Sending money to a Facebook “night officer” is not.

### Does Low Power Mode on iPhone make night failures worse?

It can delay notifications. It does not stop you from opening Messages. It does not stop USSD. The myth that Apple “blocks ECG at night” is a myth.

### Should I buy extra units in the afternoon to avoid night?

If you can afford a buffer, yes. That is cheaper than night-vendor premium and cheaper than double-pays. The app is happier when you are happier.

Night breaks apps before it breaks shortcodes. One debit, one token, one walk to the CIU. Save the heroics for the morning update.
