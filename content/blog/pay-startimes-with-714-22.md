StarTimes is a third TV biller on the same Ewale TV menu. The number on a DSTV card will not service a StarTimes box. Read the logo on the decoder before you pick the row.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The **StarTimes smartcard / account number** on that decoder.
2. A funded wallet.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Choose **TV Bills** → **StarTimes**.
3. Enter the number exactly. Confirm.
4. Pick the bouquet or amount shown. Approve the PIN.

## After you pay

Activation can lag a few minutes. Keep SMS. Do not stack a second StarTimes payment on a slow restore.

## If something fails

- **Wrong TV brand on the menu:** Match the decoder brand to DSTV, GOtv, or StarTimes.
- **Partial digits:** Missing the last two digits is a different customer.

Brand match, number match, one PIN. That renews StarTimes from a keypad phone.
