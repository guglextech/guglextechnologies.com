Lost PIN is usually a lost message, not a lost payment. Hunt the inbox like you would hunt a scratch card under the sofa.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Access to the paying SIM.
2. MoMo date and amount.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Search the thread list for Ewale / serial / PIN.
2. Ask who else might have received “Other”.
3. Screenshot cloud backups if the phone died.
4. Support with the reference if the debit is real and the SMS never existed.

## After you pay

A second purchase is valid if support cannot rebuild the first voucher. Document that, then buy.

## If something fails

- **Factory reset:** The network may still have delivered SMS. A new phone with the same SIM can show old messages depending on the operator — look before you pay.

Find first. Repurchase last.
