No card in the house does not mean no checker. Ewale texts the two secrets the card would have hidden under latex.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A phone, not a kiosk.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Buy WASSCE on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**).
2. Treat SMS fields as the scratched panel.
3. Enter them on [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/).

## After you pay

If you find an old unused card later, you now have two vouchers. Use them for two needs, not two tries at the same panic.

## If something fails

- **Bought digital and card “to compare”:** Unnecessary unless you have two candidates.

The portal never required plastic. It required serial and PIN.
