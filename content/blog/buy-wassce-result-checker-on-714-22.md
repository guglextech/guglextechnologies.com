A WASSCE result checker is not a PDF and not the result itself. It is a voucher — serial and PIN — that you type on the WAEC result site or the checking shortcode WAEC publishes for that year.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The decision that you need **WASSCE**, not BECE.
2. A wallet that can take the amount on the confirmation screen.
3. The paying number, because that is where the SMS voucher usually arrives.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Choose **Education** / result checker.
3. Select **WASSCE**. Do not pick BECE to “try”.
4. Confirm the amount. Approve the PIN.
5. Keep the SMS. It contains the serial and PIN.

## After you pay

Use the voucher on WAEC’s official checker. Ewale does not host results. One voucher is typically one full result check as WAEC defines it that year.

## Index number vs checker

Your index number identifies the candidate. The checker voucher pays for access. You need both. Ewale sells the voucher, not the index number.

## If something fails

- **Bought BECE by mistake:** That voucher will not open WASSCE results. Buy WASSCE as a new product.
- **No SMS:** Wait, check the inbox, then support with the debit reference. Do not buy a second voucher immediately.

Finish one exam type. Save the SMS before you delete threads.
