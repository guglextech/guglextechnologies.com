Ghana Water’s customer ledger is not Ewale’s ledger. They update on different clocks. That is the entire issue behind “I paid but the bill is the same.”

## What “successful” means on Ewale

On **\*714\*22#**, success means:

- You confirmed the account.
- Mobile money **debited**.
- Ewale recorded a **paid** collection against that attempt.

It does **not** mean Ghana Water has printed a new bill or refreshed a balance API.

## What Ghana Water is doing

Many utility postings run in **batches**. A payment received in the morning may appear on the account in the afternoon, overnight, or the next working cycle. Field disconnection lists can lag as well — which is why you should still carry the **reference** if a crew arrives the same day.

## How to check without paying again

1. Mobile money: is there a completed **debit** for that amount and time?
2. SMS: is there an Ewale or wallet confirmation with a **reference**?
3. Wait through the posting window. Do not treat “bill unchanged after 20 minutes” as failure.

If (1) is missing, the payment did not complete. Dial again.

If (1) is present, a second *714*22# payment is a **new** payment.

## Talking to Ghana Water with proof

Desk staff will not reconstruct a USSD session. They need:

- Account number.
- Date, time, amount.
- Mobile money receipt / Ewale reference.

Pay from the number you can prove. Paying from a friend’s wallet without keeping the SMS makes the later argument harder.

## Disconnection and same-day payment

If you pay to avoid disconnection, pay early enough for posting — or be ready to show the reference on site. Ewale moving the money is necessary. It is not always sufficient *that hour* on Ghana Water’s field list.

## Product language that matters

Silence (“we will update shortly” with no window) looks like theft. A clear line — payment received, bill may take up to a day to show it — is the honest description of this rail.

Use *714*22# for collection. Use the reference for everything after.
