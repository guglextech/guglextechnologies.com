USSD will accept a well-formed number that is not yours. Finding the physical card takes two minutes and saves a month of someone else’s Compact.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Access to the decoder.
2. A photo if you pay remotely for family.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Look on the smartcard (pull it if needed) and on the decoder label.
2. If you pay from afar, ask for a photo of that label, not a number typed from memory.
3. Enter it under **TV Bills** → **DSTV** on **\*714\*22#**.

## After you pay

Save the photo in the family chat with the word DSTV so nobody pastes a GOtv IUC next month.

## If you cannot find it

Multichoice Ghana can retrieve it. Ewale can only charge the number you type.

## If something fails

- **Old decoder, new card:** Use the card that is actually in the slot now.
- **Installation SMS from 2019:** Verify against the hardware. Numbers get replaced.

Photograph the card once. Pay against that photo forever after.
