Networks stall. Results-week SMS storms stall more. The rule is the same as ECG tokens: do not double-pay while the first debit is live.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. MoMo transaction ID.
2. The paying number’s inbox, including filtered folders.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Search SMS for “serial”, “PIN”, or Ewale.
2. Wait a few minutes. Toggle airplane mode.
3. If still empty, support via **\*714\*22#** or walletewale@gmail.com with the reference.
4. Do not start a second identical checkout “to be safe”.

## After you pay

When the SMS lands, proceed to WAEC. The delay did not cancel the voucher.

## If something fails

- **Bought on a friend’s phone:** The SMS may be on their SIM. Call them.

Trace the first debit. Second vouchers are for second needs.
