Every ECG prepaid token is bound to a **meter number**. Ewale cannot “redirect” a token after you have paid. Confirmation happens *before* the mobile money prompt — or not at all.

## Where the number is

On most prepaid meters the number is on a **sticker** on the meter body or cover. It is not the serial on the cardboard box the meter arrived in years ago. It is not your house number.

Copy it in daylight. Worn stickers lose a digit. A missing digit still looks like a valid meter to a lookup that returns *someone*.

## What Ewale shows you on *714*22#

After you enter the meter number, Ewale looks it up and should show a **registered name**. That name is your last check.

- If it is your name, or the landlord / spouse you expect, continue.
- If it is a stranger, stop. Re-read the sticker. Try again. Do not confirm because the amount is small.

Small amounts still buy a token for the wrong meter.

## Shared meters and compounds

One meter can serve several rooms. Paying “your share” on Ewale still loads **that** meter. Agree whose name appears on the lookup, and who keeps the SMS with the token.

Do not invent a second meter number because two households share the bill. If there is one keypad, there is one meter.

## Numbers people type by mistake

- ECG **account / contract** numbers from old postpaid bills.
- A neighbour’s meter on the same pole.
- The last token (a 20-digit string) instead of the meter number.
- A phone number.

The lookup is there to catch these. Use it. If lookup fails, you do not have a valid prepaid meter on the file Ewale is querying — you do not have a payment to make yet.

## After a wrong payment

If you confirmed a wrong name and paid, the token belongs to that meter. Ewale cannot pull units back from ECG’s token system as if they were an app credit.

Contact Ewale support with the **reference**, the **meter you intended**, and the **meter that was paid**. Resolution, if any, is a support process — not a second tap on *714*22#.

The cheap fix is upstream: read the sticker, confirm the name, then approve the PIN.
