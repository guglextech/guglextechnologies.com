Quantity is a multiplier. 3 × the unit price is 3 vouchers. It is not “three extra uses” of one PIN.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A number of distinct candidates.
2. Balance for the **total**.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Choose one exam type per checkout if the menu is per-product.
2. Enter quantity.
3. Read the total twice.
4. Store every serial/PIN pair with a name.

## After you pay

If SMS shows one pair but you paid for ten, that is a support case with the debit amount — not a shrug.

## If something fails

- **Quantity 10 for one child:** Unless you need ten separate vouchers, you overpaid.

Count people, then count vouchers. Then count cedis.
