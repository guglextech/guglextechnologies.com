Parents ask for a hard copy for the house. That copy is a print of WAEC’s page, not a new card.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The result showing on the official site.
2. A clear screenshot of the full grade list.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Check BECE after buying on **\*714\*22#**.
2. Screenshot every subject.
3. Print at a shop. Do not dictate your PIN to the attendant if the page is already on your phone.

## After you pay

Keep the SMS even after printing. Reprinting later may need another allowed use of the same voucher.

## If something fails

- **Blurred photo:** Zoom and recapture. A café cannot typeset WAEC grades from memory.

Phone check first. Paper second. New checker only if WAEC requires a new voucher.
