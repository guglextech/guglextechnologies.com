Treat “invalid” as a checklist. Buying in a loop is how results week becomes expensive.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The original Ewale SMS.
2. The exact URL you are posting to.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Confirm WASSCE vs BECE vs the voucher you bought.
2. Confirm [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/) vs [eresults.waecgh.org](https://eresults.waecgh.org/).
3. Re-copy serial and PIN with no spaces.
4. Only if the debit produced **no** SMS, contact Ewale support with the reference.

## Used PIN

If a sibling already submitted successfully, the PIN did work. Check their screenshot.

## If something fails

- **Third purchase while angry:** Pause. You may already own working unused vouchers in SMS.

Debug the form before you debit again.
