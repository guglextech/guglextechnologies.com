“Check WASSCE results” is two jobs. First you buy a WAEC voucher. Then you type that voucher on the official WAEC Ghana portal. Ewale does the first job. WAEC does the second.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A **WASSCE** checker — not a BECE voucher.
2. The candidate’s **index number** (usually 10 digits).
3. A browser for [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/).

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#** or open [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com).
2. Open **Results Vouchers / Education** and choose **WASSCE / Nov-Dec**.
3. Confirm the cedi amount on the summary. Approve **one** MoMo PIN.
4. Copy **serial** and **PIN** from the SMS.
5. On [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/), enter index number, exam type, year, serial, and PIN.

## After you pay

Results display on WAEC’s site, not in the Ewale app. Save or print the page. Do not buy a second checker because the portal was slow.

## School vs private

Pick **WASSCE (School)** or **WASSCE (Private / Nov-Dec)** on the portal to match how the candidate registered. The Ewale voucher type is the same WASSCE / Nov-Dec checker.

## If something fails

- **Invalid PIN:** Swap serial and PIN fields, drop spaces, and confirm the year is 2026 (or the year they sat).
- **Lookalike website:** Stop. Prefer the address WAEC publishes. Fake pages harvest vouchers.

Voucher from Ewale. Grades from WAEC. One confirmed debit unless you truly need a second candidate.
