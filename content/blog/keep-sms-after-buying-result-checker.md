Ewale does not print a plastic card. The SMS is the card. Clearing “all messages” the night before results is how families lose paid vouchers.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The original SMS.
2. A second copy: screenshot, notebook, or a trusted person.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. After **\*714\*22#** succeeds, open SMS immediately.
2. Write serial and PIN down.
3. Do not factory-reset the phone until the result is checked.

## After you pay

If the SMS is gone but MoMo shows a debit, support may help with the reference. That is slower than simply keeping the text.

## If something fails

- **Shared inbox:** Another family member may delete “promotions.” Move the SMS or copy it out.
- **Screenshot only:** Better than nothing. Crop so serial and PIN stay readable.

The debit bought a voucher. The voucher lives in that SMS until you copy it elsewhere.
