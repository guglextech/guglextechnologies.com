Timeouts happen in traffic, in buildings, and when the wallet SIM is in another room. The repair is not always a new USSD session.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The paying number’s history.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Find the phone that owns the wallet.
2. Look for a debit.
3. Retry Ewale only if there is no debit (or there is a reverse).

## After you pay

If you approved a late prompt and also started a new session, you may have paid twice. History will show two rows.

## If something fails

- **Prompt still sitting unopened:** Open it. Do not start a twin session beside it.
- **Wrong SIM in the phone you are staring at:** The prompt followed the wallet number.

Timeout is a question: did money move? Answer that before you dial.
