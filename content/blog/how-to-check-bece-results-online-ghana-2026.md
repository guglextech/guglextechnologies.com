BECE results are not CSSPS placement. The checker voucher shows WAEC grades. School placement is a different, usually free, portal.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A **BECE** checker voucher from **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**).
2. The JHS candidate **index number**.
3. The official BECE checker: [eresults.waecgh.org](https://eresults.waecgh.org/) (or the URL WAEC publishes that year).

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**. Choose **Results Vouchers**, then **BECE**.
2. Pay once. Wait for serial and PIN by SMS.
3. Open [eresults.waecgh.org](https://eresults.waecgh.org/). Select BECE School or BECE Private.
4. Enter index number, year **2026**, serial, and PIN. Submit once.

## After you pay

If grades show, screenshot and print. Placement comes later on [cssps.gov.gh](https://www.cssps.gov.gh/) and does not consume this voucher.

## Café printing

You can check on a phone, then send the screenshot to a café. You do not need to buy the checker at the café if you already paid on Ewale.

## If something fails

- **Bought WASSCE instead:** That PIN will not open BECE. Buy BECE as a new product.
- **Two parents paid:** You now have two vouchers. Use one. Keep the other for a sibling or a second allowed check.

BECE on the Ewale row. BECE on the WAEC form. Read both words before the PIN.
