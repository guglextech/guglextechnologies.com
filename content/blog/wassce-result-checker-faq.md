This FAQ is about buying a WASSCE voucher on Ewale and checking on WAEC. It is not a place to change grades.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. WASSCE as the exam.
2. Patience for SMS after one debit.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Buy: **\*714\*22#** → WASSCE / Nov-Dec.
2. Check: [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/).
3. Keep SMS until you have a copy of the result.

## FAQ

### Does Ewale show my WASSCE grades?

No. Ewale sends serial and PIN. Grades render on WAEC’s portal.

### Can I check last year’s WASSCE?

Usually yes if you pick the correct year and index on ghana.waecdirect.org and the voucher is still valid for that lookup.

### School or Nov-Dec?

Same Ewale WASSCE / Nov-Dec product. On WAEC, choose the sitting you registered for.

### How many times can I submit?

WAEC commonly allows a few uses on the same index and year. It is not a licence for classmates.

## If something fails

- **BECE voucher in the SMS:** Wrong menu. Buy WASSCE.

Buy once. Check on WAEC. Ask Ewale only about the debit and the SMS.
