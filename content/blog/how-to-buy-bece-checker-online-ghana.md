JHS parents still hear “go and buy a card.” You can buy the same class of WAEC voucher online on Ewale without standing at a booth.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Certainty it is **BECE**, not WASSCE.
2. MoMo PIN on the paying phone.
3. A plan for who keeps the SMS — parent or candidate.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**. Open **Results Vouchers**.
2. Choose **BECE checker**. Confirm the product name.
3. Enter quantity 1 unless you are buying for more than one candidate.
4. Approve once. Copy serial and PIN from SMS.

## After you pay

Use the voucher on the official BECE checking site. Ewale will not display the grades inside USSD.

## If something fails

- **Vendor already sold you a scratch card:** Try that card first. A second digital voucher is a second fee.

Online is not a different exam. It is a different way to receive serial and PIN.
