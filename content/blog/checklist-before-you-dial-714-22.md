Every guide on this blog is a variation of the same list. Run it in thirty seconds and you avoid the expensive chapters.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The **product** name (airtime, data, DSTV, GOtv, StarTimes, ECG prepaid, ECG postpaid, Ghana Water, WASSCE, BECE, merchant).
2. The **identifier** from the physical object.
3. Enough **wallet** for the confirmation total.
4. One **human** allowed to pay.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Name the product out loud.
2. Read the identifier from hardware or a photo.
3. Dial **\*714\*22#** and open only that row.
4. Confirm name/number. Approve one PIN. Save SMS. Stop.

## After you pay

If anything in the list was missing, abort. Missing data is cheaper than a wrong success.

## Support

When it still fails after one honest debit: **\*714\*22#** Support or **walletewale@gmail.com**, reference included, PIN never included.

## If something fails

- **Doing it from memory:** Memory is how wrong meters get tokens.
- **A second person “helping” on their phone:** Helping often means a second debit.

Checklist, one sale, one SMS. That is Ewale used well.
