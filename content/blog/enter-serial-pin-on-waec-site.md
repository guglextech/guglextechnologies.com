Most “invalid PIN” errors are field swaps: serial in the PIN box, index in the serial box, or a letter O typed as zero. Slow down.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The Ewale SMS.
2. The index number on the candidate’s admission papers.
3. WAEC’s official checking page for that exam.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Copy serial exactly, including leading zeros.
2. Copy PIN exactly.
3. Enter the index number in the index field only.
4. Submit once. If it fails, re-read the fields before buying another voucher.

## After you pay

Ewale support cannot reset a PIN you already successfully used on WAEC. They can help if you never received serial and PIN after a real debit.

## If something fails

- **Lookalike website:** Stop. You may have given the voucher away. Use WAEC’s published address.
- **Spaces and hyphens:** Type only the characters in the SMS.

Match labels to SMS fields. Buy a second checker only after you are sure the first voucher never worked.
