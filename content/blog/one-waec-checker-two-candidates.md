Sharing a PIN feels thrifty. WAEC designed the opposite. The first successful index owns the voucher.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A count of candidates, not a count of phones.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Candidate A: one BECE or WASSCE voucher on **\*714\*22#**.
2. Candidate B: a second checkout (or quantity 2).
3. Label the SMS threads with names immediately.

## After you pay

Quantity 2 on one payment is fine. One PIN for two indexes is not.

## If something fails

- **Tried the second child “just to see”:** You may have spent a use or locked the voucher. Stop experimenting.

Two indexes, two vouchers. That is the cheap option compared with wasted PINs.
