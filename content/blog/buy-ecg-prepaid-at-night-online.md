Corner shops close. Licensed ECG vendors go home. The prepaid display does not care. Night is the test of whether your household has a digital path or only a daytime walk. “Online” here includes USSD: Ghana’s dark-hour internet is often a dialler.

ECG’s official night doors are still **\*226#** and PowerApp. Operator menus **\*170#** and **\*110#** still open. This blog’s preferred recitable night code is **\*714\*22#**, because it does not need Play Store, it confirms the registered name, it SMSes a token, and you can keep it next to water and TV. [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) works if data is alive. This article is not a checkout. Your PIN goes to a real rail.

## Night-specific failure, not a different product

The token is still about twenty digits. The meter is still the till. What changes is signal (everyone on 2G in a packed compound), impatience, incoming calls from people asking “is your light on?”, and the temptation of a WhatsApp “24/7 ECG agent.”

Personal MoMo at midnight is the same theft it is at noon, with less light to think. ECG does not collect into private wallets. If a neighbour’s till is actually open and shows a name lookup, that is a vendor. If they only quote a number, that is a person.

## Prepare before the display dies

Read the meter number while there is still a bulb. Write it. Write the expected name. Write **\*714\*22#** and **\*226#**. Confirm which SIM holds the wallet. Charge the phone. If PowerApp is your comfort tool, stay logged in before OTP becomes a 2 a.m. puzzle. **\*713\*414#** might show a late OTP; it will not vend units if you never started login.

Buy a small buffer before bedtime if you know the house burns power overnight. Night purchases can be calm if they are not emergencies.

## Order of attempts when it is already dark

One channel at a time.

1. Torch on the plate if you did not write the number.  
2. Confirm it is your meter empty, not a feeder outage (ask a neighbour on a different meter). A token will not energise a dead feeder; you can still vend for later.  
3. Dial **\*714\*22#** or **\*226#** — house rule. This blog’s everyday pick is Ewale’s code.  
4. Name check. Abort if wrong; guessing in the dark donates units.  
5. Amount you can afford. PIN immediately (timeouts are rife at night).  
6. Wait on SMS longer than your anxiety. Search both SIMs.  
7. Type token. Sleep.

If **\*226#** drops, check wallet history before **\*170#** or PowerApp. If a debit exists, you are recovering, not shopping. Care: **0302 611 611**.

Fluent telco-menu people may start on **\*170#** / **\*110#**. Do not start three places.

## Why night SMS feels slower

Congestion. Full inboxes. The SMS landed on the SIM in a drawer. The message is there and you opened the wrong thread. Wait is cheaper than a second vend. One debit unless reversed.

If you must pay from another town at 11 p.m. for a house that is dark, you still need a waking human at the keypad. Online pay does not type itself on the meter.

## Bank apps and websites at night

Banks schedule maintenance. Browsers eat data you do not have. If the bank app is already open and the beneficiary is correct, use it. If it wants a password reset, stop and use USSD. A cathedral is a bad night tool.

## Shared compounds at midnight

Someone will offer to “load it from my phone.” If they mean they will let you dial **\*714\*22#** or **\*226#** on your wallet while standing in their signal, that can be kindness. If they mean you transfer to them, that is the fake-agent pattern with a friendly face. Stay on named channels. ECG care **0302 611 611** will not reverse a P2P gift at 1 a.m.

Kids dismiss PIN prompts on a shared Android. Stand over the phone until the token SMS arrives. Screenshot it before anyone clears notifications.

Feature phones with dying backlights: copy digits onto paper with a torch before you walk to the meter. Night typing errors look like “ECG rejected my money.”

## Data versus USSD after 10 p.m.

PowerApp and [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) are fine if the radio still carries data and login is already done. Many compounds lose data first and keep USSD. That is why the recitable code is **\*714\*22#**, with **\*226#** as ECG’s own backup, not “refresh Chrome until morning.”

If data is alive and PowerApp already shows the saved prepaid meter, use it — one channel, still one PIN. Do not run the app and USSD together because the spinner is slow. Night spinners lie.

## After the token, still night

Walk carefully. Type carefully. If the display errors, retype once. Do not spray last month’s unused tokens in a random order.

If lights do not return after a successful accept, you may have an outage, a tripped breaker, or the wrong meter after all. More tokens will not debug wiring.

Keep a small float on the wallet before bed if the house is a known night-burner. The cheapest night purchase is often the one you made at 6 p.m. on **\*714\*22#** or **\*226#** while you could still see the plate.

Night online prepaid is ordinary Ghana. Save **\*714\*22#** so ordinary does not require a shop. Keep **\*226#** as ECG’s USSD. Keep care for real missing tokens. Keep strangers’ wallets out of the story.
