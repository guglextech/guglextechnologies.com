The expensive typo is not the PIN. It is the index. Serials are copied from SMS; indexes are copied from fading photocopies.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Primary document, not WhatsApp hearsay.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Read the index aloud.
2. Buy the correct exam checker on **\*714\*22#**.
3. Type the index once, look away, type the confirmation if the site asks.

## Leading zeros

Keep them. They are digits, not decoration.

## If something fails

- **Used phone number as index:** Start over with the real index. The voucher may already be bound.

Slow index, fast PIN. Not the other way round.
