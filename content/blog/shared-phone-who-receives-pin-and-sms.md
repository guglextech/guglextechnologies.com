A living-room phone can hold three SIMs’ worth of confusion. Ewale follows numbers, not the person holding the plastic.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Knowledge of which SIM has the wallet.
2. A rule: one person handles PIN for this bill.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Say who is paying.
2. Make sure that SIM can receive the prompt.
3. Copy token/serial out of SMS before the next person deletes “junk.”

## After you pay

If two people approve two prompts, you bought twice. If nobody copies the SMS, you bought once and threw away the product.

## If something fails

- **PIN on SIM A, USSD on SIM B:** That can still work. Watch SIM A for the prompt.
- **Inbox shared with promotions:** Search for Ewale, token, serial, DSTV, ECG.

Numbers receive messages. People must assign roles.
