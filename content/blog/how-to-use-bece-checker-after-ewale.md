After MoMo, nothing happens until a browser talks to WAEC. That is normal.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. BECE SMS.
2. Quiet so you do not mistype the index.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Open the official BECE checker ([eresults.waecgh.org](https://eresults.waecgh.org/) or WAEC’s published URL).
2. Confirm BECE School or Private.
3. Paste serial and PIN. Submit.

## After you pay

If WAEC says used, someone already checked — often another relative. Ask the family before buying again.

## If something fails

- **Captcha failed:** Retry captcha, not the purchase.

The checker is used on WAEC. Ewale already did its part.
