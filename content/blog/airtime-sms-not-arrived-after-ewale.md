SMS is the receipt, not the airtime itself. Networks sometimes credit the line before the text lands — or the text lands in a full inbox. Buying a second pack “just in case” creates two credits.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The **MoMo history** row for the debit.
2. Access to the destination phone’s balance USSD.
3. The reference if you contact support.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Do not dial **\*714\*22#** to buy again yet.
2. Check the destination airtime balance.
3. Check SMS and the SIM inbox.
4. If the balance did not move after a fair wait, use Ewale support from the USSD menu or email **walletewale@gmail.com** with the reference.

## After you pay

Support can trace a completed debit. They cannot un-send a second successful top-up you made while waiting.

## If something fails

- **Wrong destination:** The credit is on the number you confirmed. That is not a missing SMS.
- **Roaming or no signal:** SMS may queue. Balance USSD is the better check when the phone has coverage.

One debit, one credit. Prove the first sale failed before you start a second.
