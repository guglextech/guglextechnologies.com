An MTN data bundle is a pack with a size and a validity window. Buying airtime and hoping it becomes data will not attach a bundle. Use the data menu.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The **MTN number** that should receive the bundle.
2. The pack you want — daily, weekly, or monthly as shown on the menu.
3. Wallet balance that covers the pack price on the confirmation screen.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Choose **Telco** → **Data bundles** → **MTN**.
3. Enter the number, then pick the bundle from the list.
4. Confirm size, validity, amount, and number.
5. Approve the MoMo PIN.

## After you pay

The bundle sits on that MTN line. Ask the person to toggle data off and on if it does not show immediately. Do not buy a second pack while you wait two minutes.

## If something fails

- **Bought airtime by mistake:** That sale is airtime. Buy data as a new transaction on the data row.
- **Bundle not showing:** Wait, then check *131*1# or the MTN balance USSD before repeating.

Data and airtime are different products on the same shortcode. Pick the row that matches what you want to land on the SIM.
