Campus is far. MoMo is local. SMS forwards well. That is the whole long-distance checker story.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Student’s index, photographed clearly.
2. A channel to send serial and PIN (not Facebook comments).

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Buy WASSCE on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**).
2. Send SMS image on WhatsApp.
3. Student checks once and sends the result screenshot back.

## After you pay

If two uncles pay, the student should use the first working PIN and tell the family.

## If something fails

- **Sent PIN in a 40-person group:** Anyone can burn it. Use a direct chat.

Distance is not a reason to use a fixer. It is a reason to use USSD.
