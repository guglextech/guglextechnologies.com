Buying a WASSCE checker online means paying for a serial and PIN, not uploading results. Ewale is the checkout. ghana.waecdirect.org is where you later view grades.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Any Ghana GSM phone, or data for [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com).
2. Mobile Money with enough balance for the amount on screen (WAEC checkers on Ewale are shown in cedis before you approve).
3. The phone that should receive the voucher SMS.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#** or use the Education / result-checker flow on the web.
2. Select **WASSCE / Nov-Dec**, not BECE.
3. Choose self or another number if the menu offers it.
4. Read the total. Approve the MoMo prompt **once**.

## After you pay

The SMS is the card. Screenshot it. Then check on [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/) with the index number.

## If something fails

- **PIN timed out:** If MoMo did not debit, nothing was bought. Dial again.
- **Web tab closed:** Check SMS and wallet history before starting a second checkout.

Online buy, SMS voucher, official portal. That is the whole path.
