Mobile money can show a debit while Ewale is still issuing the voucher. That in-between state looks like failure. It is usually the first sale still working.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The debit reference.
2. The time you paid.
3. Access to SMS and, if you have data, the Ewale web history.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Wait several minutes. Results-week traffic is real.
2. Search SMS for Ewale, serial, or PIN.
3. If nothing arrives after a reasonable wait, use **\*714\*22#** Support or **walletewale@gmail.com** — not a second Education purchase.

## After you pay

When the voucher arrives late, you already paid. Using it is the next step. Buying again is how people end up with two PINs and a thinner wallet.

## If something fails

- **Pending then success:** Use the voucher you got. Ignore the urge to “complete” it with another debit.
- **Pending then reverse:** If the wallet reverses, the voucher was not delivered. Then you may buy again.

Pending means wait. Reverse means retry. Success means stop buying.
