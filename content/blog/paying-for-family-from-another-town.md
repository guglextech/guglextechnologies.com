Distance is not a technical problem. Duplicate pay is the social problem. The person standing next to the decoder cannot see your MoMo SMS unless you send it.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A labelled photo of the identifier.
2. A chat that one person is the payer today.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Collect the photo.
2. Pay the matching Ewale row.
3. Forward SMS. For prepaid, they still type the token.

## After you pay

If they also visited an agent because they did not see your message, you both succeeded. Put the protocol in the family group: “I pay, I send SMS, nobody else pays.”

## If something fails

- **Voice note numbers:** Always fail eventually. Use photos.
- **Paying yesterday’s meter:** Landlords change hardware. Refresh the photo.

Remote pay is easy. Remote coordination is the actual work.
