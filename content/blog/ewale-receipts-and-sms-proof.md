USSD screens disappear. Browser tabs close. SMS stays — until someone deletes it. For prepaid tokens and result checkers, the SMS is not only proof; it is the payload.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. An inbox with space.
2. A habit of screenshotting tokens and serials.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Complete the payment.
2. Open SMS before you do anything else.
3. Store the reference, token, or serial off the phone if the device is shared.

## After you pay

Support will ask for the reference. A memory of “I paid around 4 p.m.” is weaker than the SMS.

## If something fails

- **No SMS, debit present:** Use support with the wallet reference. Do not assume the product failed until they say so — and do not repurchase in the meantime.
- **Forwarded SMS got cropped:** Ask for the original. Missing digits break tokens and PINs.

If it arrived by SMS, treat that message as the official copy.
