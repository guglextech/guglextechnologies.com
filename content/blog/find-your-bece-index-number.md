Parents searching “find BECE index number” are one step from checking. The other step is the voucher. Do not pay a stranger to “locate” an index that the school already printed.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The candidate’s school contact or slip.
2. Date of birth for CSSPS later — not required to buy a checker.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Ask the JHS for the WAEC index.
2. Write it with a zero if it starts with zero.
3. Buy BECE on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**).
4. Enter that index on the official BECE portal.

## After you pay

For placement you will type index + year suffix on [cssps.gov.gh](https://www.cssps.gov.gh/). That is still not a paid checker.

## If something fails

- **Guessing the last digits:** WAEC will not correct you. You may burn a voucher use.

School has the index. Ewale has the voucher. You bring both to WAEC’s page.
