Paying utilities for someone else is normal. The failure mode is using **your** memory of **their** identifiers, then receiving the token on **your** phone while they stand at a meter you have never seen.

## ECG prepaid for someone else

You need:

- Their **meter number**, copied from the sticker, not from chat guesswork.
- To confirm the **name** on Ewale lookup.
- A plan for **who enters the token**.

The token SMS typically lands on **your** paying line. You must send them the digits, or they must be with you. If they cannot receive your WhatsApp, read the token slowly on a call and have them repeat it.

Do not pay, then travel, then remember they have no signal. The token is in your inbox.

## Ghana Water for someone else

You need the **account number** on their bill and the lookup **name**. Pay from your mobile money; keep the **reference**. Send them that reference so they can face Ghana Water without you.

You will not appear as the account holder. You are a third-party payer. That is fine. The account still gets the credit when Ghana Water posts.

## Whose wallet

Use a wallet with balance and a working PIN prompt. If you use their phone to dial **\*714\*22#**, the prompt may hit **their** wallet. If you dial from your phone, it hits **yours**.

Decide before you start. Mixed assumptions produce pending prompts on a phone in another room.

## Do not save the wrong meter as a favourite in your head

If you pay two households, label notes “Maame — meter …” and “Tema — meter …”. Paying the Tema meter against a Kasoa outage is a complete, irreversible token for the wrong city.

## Aftercare

Tell them:

- ECG: wait for you to send the token; do not buy a second one.
- Water: wait for posting; do not pay again because the bill PDF is unchanged this evening.

You are the one with the reference. Treat that as responsibility, not as a favour that ends at the PIN.
