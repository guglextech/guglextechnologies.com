People search “buy results” and expect a file. What Ewale sells is closer to the old scratch card: a serial and a PIN. Until you submit those to WAEC, you have not seen the result.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The SMS from Ewale with **serial** and **PIN**.
2. The candidate **index number**.
3. WAEC’s official result site or checking channel for that exam year.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Buy the correct exam voucher on **\*714\*22#**.
2. Wait for SMS. Write the serial and PIN on paper if the phone is shared.
3. Open WAEC’s checker. Enter index number, serial, and PIN as labelled.
4. Do not type the Ewale payment reference into WAEC. That reference is for support, not for results.

## After you pay

If WAEC says the PIN is used or invalid, stop buying more Ewale vouchers until you confirm you are on the official checker and the exam type matches.

## If something fails

- **Used PIN:** A voucher that already returned a result will not work as a second card.
- **Wrong site:** Lookalike result websites collect vouchers. Prefer WAEC’s published URL.

Ewale sells access. WAEC holds the grades. Keep the SMS until the candidate has a copy of the result they need.
