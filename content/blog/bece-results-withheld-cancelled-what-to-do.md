The checker did its job if it showed a withhold. Buy another checker only if you never saw any official status.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The screenshot of what WAEC displayed.
2. The school’s contact for withheld cases.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Do not pay a fixer.
2. Ask the school / WAEC channels published for that year.
3. Keep the Ewale SMS as proof you checked.

## After you pay

Placement may also stall. That is CSSPS/GES, still not a second voucher.

## If something fails

- **Someone selling “release codes”:** Scam. Stop.

You paid to see the truth. The next step is official, not another USSD panic.
