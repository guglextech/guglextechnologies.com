The power of one shortcode is also the trap: every identifier looks like “just numbers.” Meter, IUC, smartcard, phone, GWCL account, and wallet numbers are not interchangeable.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A label on the paper in your hand: what this number is.
2. The matching menu row.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Decide the product.
2. Open only that row on **\*714\*22#**.
3. Paste only the identifier that belongs there.
4. Confirm the name or number preview.

## After you pay

If you started ECG and meant DSTV, cancel before PIN. After PIN, you bought ECG.

## If something fails

- **Muscle memory:** Last night’s meter number will still be in your head. Clear it before TV bills.
- **Same session, two products:** Finish one SMS, then start the next bill. Sessions are cheap. Confusion is not.

One shortcode, one product at a time, one matching number.
