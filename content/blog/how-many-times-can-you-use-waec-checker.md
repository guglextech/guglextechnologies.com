Reprint, yes. New cousin, no. If you need another person checked, buy another voucher on Ewale.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The same index and year as the first successful check.
2. The same serial and PIN.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Reuse on the official portal until WAEC refuses.
2. Screenshot early so you are not dependent on leftover uses.
3. Buy a fresh checker on **\*714\*22#** only when WAEC says the voucher is exhausted or you have a new candidate.

## After you pay

Ewale cannot add extra uses to a PIN WAEC already issued.

## If something fails

- **Portal down, bought another:** Wait. Uses remaining on the first PIN still matter.

A few looks at one result. Not a neighbourhood licence.
