Paying from your MoMo does not force the credit onto your SIM. Ewale asks for a destination number. That is the only number that receives the airtime.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The other person’s **full number**, not a contact nickname.
2. The correct **network** row (MTN, Telecel, AT, or Glo).
3. A way to tell them the SMS arrived so they do not buy a second pack.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#** or open [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com).
2. Pick **Airtime** and the destination network.
3. Type their number. Read it back on the confirm screen.
4. Pay with your wallet. Keep the SMS for both of you.

## After you pay

If they say the credit is missing, check the number on your SMS before you top up again. A successful debit to the wrong line is not a failed payment.

## If something fails

- **Saved contact was stale:** USSD does not use your phonebook. Type the digits.
- **They already bought airtime:** Two successful debits are two credits. Coordinate before repeating.

The summary screen is the product. If the number there is theirs, you are done after one PIN.
