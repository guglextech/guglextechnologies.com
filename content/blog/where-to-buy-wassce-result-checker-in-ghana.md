“Where to buy WASSCE checker” usually means: who is allowed to sell a WAEC voucher, and how fast you get serial and PIN. Ewale is a digital counter that is open when the street stall is not.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A decision: shop card, or digital on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**).
2. Time on results week — shops run out; USSD does not need shelf stock.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. If you want digital: dial **\*714\*22#**, WASSCE / Nov-Dec, pay once.
2. If you already hold an unused scratch card, use that first.
3. Check only on [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/).

## After you pay

Wherever you bought it, WAEC sees a serial and PIN. They do not see your shop name.

## Do not buy from result-upgrade pages

Anyone offering to change grades is not selling a checker. WAEC has warned against that trade. Buy a voucher, then check yourself.

## If something fails

- **Sold a screenshot of someone else’s PIN:** That voucher may already be used. Buy from Ewale so the SMS is issued to you.

A trusted checkout plus the official portal beats a stranger’s WhatsApp “checker”.
