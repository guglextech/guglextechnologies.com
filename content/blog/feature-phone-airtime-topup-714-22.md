A keypad phone in a market stall can complete the same Ewale airtime sale as a smartphone. The constraint is the USSD session clock and the PIN prompt on the paying SIM.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Any GSM handset that can dial USSD.
2. The paying SIM inserted (or a wallet that receives the prompt on that number).
3. The destination number written down if you cannot open contacts mid-session.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**. Do not open WhatsApp or a browser.
2. Work the menu with the number keys.
3. When the PIN prompt arrives as a separate message, approve it. That prompt is the payment.
4. Wait for the SMS receipt even if the USSD screen has already closed.

## After you pay

Closing the USSD screen is not the same as cancelling. If MoMo sent a prompt, check history before you assume nothing happened.

## If something fails

- **Slow feature phone:** Start again if the session dies. Check the wallet first.
- **Cannot receive SMS:** A full SIM inbox can hide receipts. Delete old messages.

No app, no data. The shortcode is the product for this phone.
