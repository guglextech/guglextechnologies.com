Paying and receiving can be different numbers. If you get that wrong, the candidate stares at an empty inbox while your phone holds the PIN.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The number that should store the voucher.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. On **\*714\*22#**, read Self vs Other carefully.
2. Other: type the candidate’s number as you would a transfer, then confirm the name if shown.
3. Pay. Confirm which SIM beeped.

## After you pay

If it landed on you, forward it. The voucher still works.

## If something fails

- **Other number with a typo:** A stranger may now hold the PIN. Support with the reference; do not spray more purchases blindly.

Name the inbox. Then name the exam. Then pay.
