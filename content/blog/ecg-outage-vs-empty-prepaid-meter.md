Empty units and a feeder outage feel the same in a dark room. One is solved with a token. The other is solved by ECG restoring the line. Buying tokens during a neighbourhood outage only stores units for later.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A look at neighbours’ lights or a compound meter.
2. The meter’s own unit display if you can reach it.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Check whether other houses have power.
2. If only you are off and the meter is at zero, buy prepaid on **\*714\*22#**.
3. If the street is off, wait on ECG. Do not panic-buy a second token.

## After you pay

Units you buy during an outage should still sit on the meter when the feeder returns — if you entered the token. Enter it when you can see the keypad.

## If something fails

- **Bought postpaid during a prepaid empty:** Wrong product. Use prepaid for tokens.
- **Token entered, still dark, street dark:** That is an outage. The token likely already sat on the meter.

Diagnose the dark before you spend. Tokens fix empty meters, not broken feeders.
