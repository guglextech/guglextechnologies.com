The child has the index. You have the wallet. Ewale lets those two facts meet without sending the child to a night market.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Your MoMo.
2. The child’s BECE index, written down.
3. Ten quiet minutes and a browser.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. You dial **\*714\*22#** and buy **BECE**.
2. You copy serial and PIN.
3. Together you open the official BECE site and submit.
4. You screenshot. You do not buy a second “just in case” the same minute.

## After you pay

Tell the other parent in the house. Duplicate checkouts are the classic results-week tax.

## If something fails

- **Uncle also bought:** Use one voucher. Save the second for a sibling or an allowed re-check.

One adult, one debit, one child, one BECE PIN — unless you have two children.
