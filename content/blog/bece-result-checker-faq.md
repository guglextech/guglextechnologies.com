Most BECE FAQ searches are the same three fears: wrong card, no SMS, and whether placement needs another payment.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The word **BECE** on the Ewale summary.
2. Index number from the JHS.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Buy on **\*714\*22#**.
2. Check on the official BECE portal.
3. Place later on [cssps.gov.gh](https://www.cssps.gov.gh/) if that is the next task.

## FAQ

### Is the checker the same as school placement?

No. Checker = WAEC grades. CSSPS = school. Do not skip the distinction on results week.

### Can two children share one PIN?

No. Once a voucher is tied to an index, it is not a family pass.

### Do I need a smartphone to buy?

No. *714*22# runs on a keypad phone. You may need a browser later to see grades.

### What if results are withheld?

That message comes from WAEC, not from Ewale. A second voucher will not lift a withhold.

## If something fails

- **Paid twice in panic:** Check both SMS threads. You may already have a working PIN.

BECE voucher, BECE portal, then CSSPS if you need the school name.
