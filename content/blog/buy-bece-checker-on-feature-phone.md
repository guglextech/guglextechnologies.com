Buying does not require Chrome. Seeing the table of subjects usually does. Split the work: buy on the Nokia, check on a neighbour’s Android.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A dialler.
2. Later, a browser you trust.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. On the feature phone, dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Buy BECE. Write serial and PIN in a notebook.
3. On any browser, open the official BECE portal and type what you wrote.

## After you pay

Do not give the notebook to a café and walk away. Stay while they type.

## If something fails

- **USSD not on that network:** Try the other SIM or the web checkout on a borrowed data phone.

Keypad can buy. A browser must show. Plan both.
