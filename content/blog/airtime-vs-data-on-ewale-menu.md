The Ewale telco menu splits airtime and data because operators settle them as different products. A successful airtime debit will not switch on a monthly bundle.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A decision before you dial: **credit** or **bundle**.
2. The destination number and its real network.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. If you want talk-time or general credit, open **Airtime**.
3. If you want internet, open **Data bundles**.
4. Finish one flow. Do not jump to the other mid-session.

## After you pay

If you bought airtime by accident, use that credit as airtime. Buy data as a second, separate payment only if you still need a bundle.

## If something fails

- **Vendor confusion:** Shop agents sometimes sell “data” from airtime menus. Ewale will not auto-convert.
- **Repeating to “fix” it:** A second debit is a second product, not a correction.

Read the menu title before the amount. That title is what you are buying.
