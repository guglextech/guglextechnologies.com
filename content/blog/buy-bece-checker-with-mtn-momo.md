If the candidate is in JHS, the voucher word on Ewale must be BECE. Paying with MTN MoMo does not change the exam type.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. MTN MoMo PIN.
2. BECE index number ready **after** purchase, on the WAEC site — not typed into Ewale.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Choose **BECE** under Results Vouchers.
3. Approve MTN MoMo once.
4. Send serial and PIN to whoever will open [eresults.waecgh.org](https://eresults.waecgh.org/).

## After you pay

Placement is not this SMS. Grades are this SMS plus the index number.

## If something fails

- **Candidate sat WASSCE:** Stop. Buy WASSCE instead. MoMo will not convert the voucher.

MTN is the rail. BECE is the product. Keep them straight on the summary.
