Consumers buy value. Merchants collect and settle. Both may see **\*714\*22#**, but a merchant withdrawal is not an ECG token and should not be treated like one.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. A merchant Ewale wallet you actually own.
2. The correct menu for **earnings / withdrawal**, not a consumer bill row.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#** or sign in on [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com).
2. Open the merchant / wallet section, not Telco or TV Bills.
3. Confirm the destination wallet or bank details Ewale shows.
4. Approve the prompt. Keep the receipt.

## After you pay

Settlement timing depends on the rail. A pending payout is not a reason to run the same withdrawal twice.

## If something fails

- **Used the airtime menu:** That buys airtime. It does not withdraw merchant float.
- **Duplicate withdraw:** Check ledger first. Two successes are two payouts.

Know whether you are a customer buying value or a merchant moving float. The menus are different on purpose.
