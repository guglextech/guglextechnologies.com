Private BECE exists. The Ewale product is still labelled BECE. The portal asks which stream. Mixing WASSCE into this story is how PINs get wasted.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. BECE voucher.
2. The correct BECE stream on [eresults.waecgh.org](https://eresults.waecgh.org/).

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Purchase BECE on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**).
2. Select BECE (School) or BECE (Private).
3. Enter index and year. Submit.

## After you pay

CSSPS rules for private candidates can differ. Read GES notices. The checker still only shows grades.

## If something fails

- **Used a friend’s school index:** You may lock the voucher to the wrong person.

BECE voucher first. Stream second. Placement third.
