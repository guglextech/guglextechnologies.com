IUC looks like “just numbers,” same as every other bill. The decoder is the source of truth. A WhatsApp number copied three tenants ago is how the wrong box gets paid.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. Line of sight to the GOtv box.
2. A close photo for anyone paying from another town.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Read the IUC on the decoder sticker.
2. Send the photo, not only a typed string.
3. Pay **TV Bills** → **GOtv** on Ewale.

## After you pay

When SMS confirms, tell the house so they do not pay cash at a shop the same afternoon.

## If something fails

- **DSTV number on GOtv flow:** Different products. Different IDs.
- **Tenant left with the number:** Read the box in the room you still occupy.

IUC from this decoder, GOtv row, one payment.
