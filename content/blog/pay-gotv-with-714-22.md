GOtv is not DSTV. The identifier is usually the IUC number on the decoder. Using a DSTV smartcard on the GOtv row will fail or pay the wrong product.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The **IUC / decoder number** on the GOtv box.
2. Wallet balance for the package shown on confirmation.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#**.
2. Open **TV Bills** → **GOtv**.
3. Enter the IUC. Confirm any name or number preview.
4. Select the package the menu lists. Approve once.

## After you pay

Keep the decoder on. GOtv restore time is similar to DSTV: minutes, not always seconds. SMS is the receipt.

## Packages

Menus may list Lite, Value, Plus, Max, Supa, or similar. Prices change. Trust the amount on the confirmation screen, not a blog memory of last year’s price.

## If something fails

- **Used DSTV menu:** Cancel if you have not approved PIN. After PIN, you paid DSTV.
- **Expired IUC mix-up:** Photo the back of this decoder today, not last year’s box.

IUC in the GOtv row. One approval. Then wait on the decoder.
