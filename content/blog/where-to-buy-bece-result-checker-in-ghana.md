BECE week empties the corner shop. The product you need is still a WAEC BECE voucher. Ewale is one place that sells it to a phone.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. BECE, not WASSCE, not a CSSPS “placement card”.
2. MoMo on the paying line.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#** anywhere in Ghana the USSD works.
2. Results Vouchers → BECE.
3. Pay. Keep SMS.
4. Check on [eresults.waecgh.org](https://eresults.waecgh.org/).

## After you pay

Buying in Accra for a candidate in Wa is normal. Forward the SMS. The index number stays with the candidate.

## If something fails

- **Paid a school “collector” with no voucher:** Ask for serial and PIN in writing. If none, you did not buy a checker.

Location of the buyer does not matter. Exam type and SMS do.
