1. Scepticism.
2. The official portal only.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

2. Check yourself on [ghana.waecdirect.org](https://ghana.waecdirect.org/) or [eresults.waecgh.org](https://eresults.waecgh.org/).
3. Ignore anyone who wants MoMo to “meet WAEC staff”.

## What a real purchase looks like
