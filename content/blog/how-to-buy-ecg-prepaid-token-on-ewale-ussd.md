ECG prepaid power is sold as a **token** — a string of digits you type into the meter keypad. The money you pay is not credit sitting in an app. The product is the token, and it only exists after the payment settles.

Ewale sells those tokens on the web and on USSD **\*714\*22#**. This guide covers the USSD path, which works on any GSM phone without data.

## Before you dial

You need three things:

1. The **meter number** on the prepaid meter (usually on a sticker on the meter body).
2. A **mobile money** wallet with enough balance for the amount plus any charges your network applies.
3. The phone that will receive the **PIN prompt** — typically the number tied to that wallet.

Do not guess the meter number. A wrong meter still produces a valid token for *someone else*.

## The payment flow on *714*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#** and wait for the Ewale menu.
2. Choose **ECG** / prepaid electricity (wording on the menu may read “ECG prepaid” or “Electricity”).
3. Enter the **meter number** exactly as printed.
4. Confirm the **registered name** that comes back. If it is not your household, stop and re-enter the number.
5. Enter the **amount in Ghana cedis**.
6. Confirm the amount and the meter on the summary screen.
7. Approve the **mobile money prompt** with your PIN.

Do not hang up until you see a confirmation, or until the session says the prompt has been sent. The session and the payment are different: the USSD screen can close while the prompt is still waiting on your phone.

## What happens after you pay

A successful payment does **not** always show the token on the USSD screen. On Ewale, the token is produced after settlement and is delivered by **SMS** to the paying number.

Keep that SMS. You will need the digits at the meter. If the SMS is delayed a few minutes, wait before buying a second token — duplicate purchases are two separate tokens, not a retry of the first.

## Entering the token on the meter

On most ECG prepaid meters:

1. Wake the keypad.
2. Enter the token digits in order.
3. Press the enter / blue button as the meter expects.
4. The display should show units added.

If the meter rejects the token, check that you typed every digit, that you used the token from the latest SMS, and that you are standing at the meter you paid for.

## If something fails

- **Declined PIN:** the meter was never charged. Dial again and approve the prompt.
- **Prompt timeout:** check mobile money history before repeating. Do not assume the debit failed.
- **No SMS:** wait, then check Ewale on the web if you have data, or dial again only to look up history if the menu supports it. Do not buy a second token “just in case.”

The rule is simple: one confirmed debit, one token. Treat the SMS as the receipt.
