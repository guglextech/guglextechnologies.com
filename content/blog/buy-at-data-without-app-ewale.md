You do not need the AT app to attach a data bundle. Ewale’s USSD path runs on any GSM phone. You only need the destination number and a wallet that can approve a PIN.

Payments happen on **Ewale** ([ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com) or **\*714\*22#**), not on this blog.

## What you need

1. The **AT number**.
2. A wallet PIN you can enter before the session times out.
3. The pack as listed — do not invent a custom size unless the menu offers it.

## The flow on \*714\*22#

1. Dial **\*714\*22#** from the phone in your hand — it does not have to be the AT SIM you are crediting.
2. Choose **Data** → **AT**.
3. Enter the AT number, pick the pack, confirm, approve.

## After you pay

The buying phone can be MTN. The receiving SIM must be AT. Mix those up and you have paid for the wrong product.

## If something fails

- **Buying from an MTN phone:** That is fine. Destination number is what matters.
- **App download prompts:** Ignore them for this payment. USSD already completed the sale.

Feature phones can finish this flow. The app is optional. The PIN is not.
